## Actual solver fact being measured

At a scored beam phase, `_mechanicBucketSelect` reserves `max(1,floor(K/nbBuckets))` entries from every distinct `(mustCrossMask,flipperUsedMask)` bucket, before filling remaining positions globally by score. This can select a lower-ranked node from a sparsely represented bucket while rejecting a higher-scoring candidate ranked among global top K. That diversity tax is not intrinsically a correctness defect: preserving minority mechanic histories can improve completion. But the 2026-10-19 R02108 source case was a genuine top-K **rank329** known-valid candidate eliminated by the quota and a one-phase plain-top-K intervention preserved it through depth63. The missing issue is whether such displacement recurs broadly in *current-input* frontiers, and what its score cost looks like without retrospective path labels.

Add one optional, independent `PrepLevel._mechanicBucketOpportunityObserver` at the **actual** original bucket-selection decision. It does *not* attach the expensive all-path research observer. It reads the already score-sorted candidate pool and actually selected frontiers, recording numeric facts only: active bucket count, global-topK displaced count, top-half and top-quarter displaced count, smallest discarded global rank, outside-topK promoted count/worst rank, sum of scores lost to reserved-bucket choices, normalized score debt and native work/depth at the phase. These metrics are **causal accounting of the existing selection**, not proof a discarded path is LIVE. The hook reads no hints, does no policy evaluation, and never changes a work meter.

### Historical adverse baseline: minority-bucket diversity can be necessary elsewhere

The [2026-09-12 mechanic-bucket intersection-heavy pilot](2026-09-12-mechanic-bucket-on-intersection-heavy-canary-001.md) tested adding mechanic-bucket retention on 28 formerly plain population cells: **0 solves, 0 regressions, mean support-depth delta −0.75**, with three selected cells suffering large retention losses. It is evidence that a fixed blanket bucket policy can displace more relevant paths, but **not** evidence that removing bucket reservations from all must-cross-heavy methods is beneficial. The existing policy was promoted for rare mechanistic capability, and any quota-debt reform must quantify **both** displaced high-score histories and protected mechanic-minority candidates before claiming improved cold solve rate. The present source-only measurement changes no policy, and deliberately does not condition the denominator on known R02108 failure or these historical support-depth labels.
