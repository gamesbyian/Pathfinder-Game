## Earned and implemented

The WS2 failure-response reconnaissance contract now carries:

- a live rival set;
- target/deployment-envelope semantics;
- adaptive-decision lineage;
- separated task-framing/prompt and authority/context-exposure independence;
- explicit critical-code dependence;
- a prospective expected result shape;
- prespecified scientific surprise conditions;
- an anomaly policy that keeps protocol/integrity/instrument failures out of the scientific-surprise bucket.

These additions live inside the already-required analysis contract. No new registry, scheduler or prospective-design database was created.

## Not earned

### Hard-blind confirmation

Not implemented for this slice.

The WS2 reconnaissance is explicitly **development/discriminator-selection evidence**. Any selected descendant must freeze its own design and becomes development until a new precommitment. There is no broad promotion claim whose validity currently depends on stronger outcome isolation.

Adding a sealed confirmation workflow here would be ceremony without a decision consumer.

### Mandatory candidate/proposal registry

Not implemented.

Bundle E found sparse structured nomination provenance, but the bounded archaeology showed that four of five alarms preserved their candidate context in prose and the fifth was a closed historical form with no live consequence.

Prospective question records should preserve nomination/candidate context when it matters, using existing relation/preflight/analysis-contract surfaces. A new proposal registry is not earned.

### Universal target-envelope fields

Not implemented.

The WS2 slice has a target envelope because its result could otherwise be over-read beyond the current residual/protocol boundary. Narrow deterministic or purely forensic work should not inherit fields it cannot use.

## Disposition

Bundle F is complete for the currently earned scope.

The rule going forward remains proportional:

> stronger prospective protection follows selection pressure, inferential breadth, and demonstrated failure modes; it does not become mandatory simply because the control plane can represent it.
