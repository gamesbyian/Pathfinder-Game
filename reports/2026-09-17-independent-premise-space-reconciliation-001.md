## Why this reconciliation exists

The independent investigation intentionally quarantined the recent premise-map artifacts and reconstructed the space from solver code, history, experiments, reports, tooling, corpora and older research. That makes agreement between the two lineages useful evidence that the main conceptual gaps are not merely artifacts of one ontology.

The independent work remained a checkpointed investigation rather than a finished competing atlas. Treating its notebook as a second canonical map would therefore create duplicate authorities and make future premise archaeology harder. The correct integration unit is the surviving conceptual delta.

## Surviving independent deltas integrated as P197-P200

### P197 — information authority

The independent pass exposed a distinction that cuts across claim type and evidence state: **what is an item of information licensed to do?**

The project already contains several authorities that must not be silently collapsed:

- canonical/referee truth;
- provable legality or feasibility consequence;
- safe dominance/equivalence;
- heuristic prediction;
- empirical failed-experience evidence;
- diagnostic correlation;
- scheduler prior/routing belief;
- inherited production default.

A highly predictive observer does not thereby gain authority to prune. Conversely, a sound consequence left permanently at observer authority may represent unused capability. P197 makes authority a first-class premise dimension.

### P198 — mechanism/deployment maturity

The independent history pass repeatedly recovered a ladder:

`phenomenon exists -> mechanism represents it -> mechanism is sound/faithful -> mechanism participates in real search -> mechanism changes useful work -> mechanism adds complementary value at relevant matched cost -> scheduler can expose it economically -> production promotion`

Current doctrine protects many of these transitions separately, but the ladder itself was not explicit. P198 prevents results at one rung from being casually promoted to claims about later rungs.

### P199 — unit of generalization

Level blindness rules out identity-specific steering, but it does not imply that **static level features** are the correct generalizing object. Research currently generalizes over several different units:

- level structure/features;
- dynamic search/failure state;
- trajectory or predecessor history;
- obligation/resource configuration;
- causal first-loss class;
- population or residual regime.

P199 asks which identity-free unit actually carries reusable solver knowledge. This is especially important for routing and learning work because the wrong unit can make a genuinely general dynamic mechanism look like overfitting or can overfit surface descriptors while missing causal state.

### P200 — information half-life and transfer radius

P162/P177/P193 cover important handoff and communication descendants. The independent pass supplied the missing parent question: **for every expensive fact or artifact produced by search, how long should it remain useful and how far should it travel?**

Examples include frontiers, failed-state experience, proofs, forced relations, exact answers, elites, near-misses, telemetry, route classifications and causal explanations. The optimal lifetime need not coincide with a module boundary. Too-short lifetime causes paid-for information to be repeatedly rediscovered; too-long or over-broad transfer can preserve stale context and mislead later stages.

## Independent research-function lens retained as an audit tool

The independent branch also recovered a 13-function vocabulary. It is useful, but it should **not** replace the canonical locus/claim/scope/evidence/relation ontology. Its value is as an orthogonal audit lens for interfaces:

1. **Encode** — choose which puzzle/path/history facts exist in the decision representation.
2. **Derive** — infer legality, feasibility, bounds, topology or obligation consequences.
3. **Generate** — make successor states, mutations, seeds, policies or stage actions available.
4. **Reject** — remove alternatives by proof, dominance, merge, eligibility or exhaustion.
5. **Prefer** — score/order/rank alternatives.
6. **Retain** — preserve alternatives across width, diversity and lifecycle boundaries.
7. **Remember** — keep frontier, experience, proofs, witnesses, elites or telemetry.
8. **Allocate** — distribute canonical work among alternatives and techniques.
9. **Select** — condition which action receives work on current evidence/context.
10. **Transfer** — move useful information between stages, attempts, families or processes.
11. **Recognize** — classify solution, exhaustion, deadlock, censoring or other terminal state.
12. **Measure** — define work, progress, contribution, coverage and experimental population.
13. **Infer from evidence** — decide what proposition an observation actually supports or closes.

The useful question is not whether every function has a named module. It is whether the *interfaces among functions* hide capability loss. A fact can be correctly derived but never transferred; an alternative can be generated but systematically fail retention; a mechanism can have intrinsic reach but never receive allocation or selection.
