## Executive result

The audit asked:

> **How much expensive mathematical reasoning is Pathfinder performing redundantly, unnecessarily, at the wrong computational resolution, or after the useful decision has already been obtained?**

The main answer is not "build a cache."

The audit found one genuinely reusable exact implication, followed it from theorem to runtime recurrence to earlier applicability, and then demonstrated that the removable work was too small to justify consuming it.

The strongest surviving exact-proof opportunity is **dynamic BC1**, whose incidence is already positive but whose removable-work economics remain unknown. That work is already owned by WS2-CUT-BALANCE-PROJECTION and does not become a second queue here.

## What the audit changed

The most important architectural change is methodological.

A proof-reuse candidate must clear, in order:

1. theorem / sound semantic relation;
2. recurrence;
3. cheap selector / applicability;
4. earlier or repeated consumer;
5. **non-overlapping removable work**;
6. lookup/construction economics;
7. only then behavioral A/B.

Connectivity cut reuse passed 1-4 and failed 5-6.

That is now the template for future exact-proof work, including BC1.

## Reopen rules

Reopen computational-work-elimination as a distinct audit only if one of these occurs:

- a new exact/safe proof family shows repeated expensive derivation with a plausible cheaper consumer;
- a major solver architecture change invalidates current work/cadence economics;
- a query family repeatedly asks the same expensive exact subproblem under a proved identity/equivalence;
- new telemetry exposes substantial post-decision work that current systems cannot attribute;
- at least two concrete consumers independently need a common proof/reuse substrate.

Otherwise continue through the existing canonical workstreams rather than recreating this audit.
