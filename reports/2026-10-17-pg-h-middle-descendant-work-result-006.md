## Stop rule and surviving directions

The particular width-500 middle-counted pruning experiment has a serious work-opportunity failure and must stop before more exact-query expense. This is *not* a negative fate census: we did not ask whether the middle children are DEAD or whether some are semantically forced. The separate shallow R00242 group remains semantically positive on a single independent parent, with no proven cheap general consumer. The next experiment, if warranted, should first establish materially larger per-parent retained descendant exposure using current-solve facts or target another search width/stage, and only then compare a cheapest current-state discriminator to a bounded exact oracle. The current workstream remains active with this tested form closed.
