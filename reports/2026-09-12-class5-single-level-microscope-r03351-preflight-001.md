# Class-5 single-level microscope: R03351 preflight 001

> **Status:** prespecified / ready for execution
> **Date:** 2026-09-12
> **Purpose:** use one representative class-5 miss as a microscope to generate a new generic solver-capability premise. A solve of this one level is not itself success.
> **Specimen:** `R03351`

## Why a one-level microscope is earned now

The current production boundary leaves 431 class-5 Corpus-2 misses with no known admissible/T1 candidate. The cheap composition routes have been substantially exhausted for this cycle: the bounded capability-memory census has zero class-5 reach; naive family/reference comparison is confound-dominated; the doubly confirmed 28-level first-loss population loses known-live beam support through score-width culling; widening is insensitive; repair does not naturally approach those trajectories; and two distinct bucket-retention treatments (`intsBucketRetention` and existing `mechanicBucketRetention`) both closed negative on the frozen population.

That creates a premise-generation problem, not an evaluation-capacity problem. The next useful question can therefore be narrower: **what future-relevant property does a genuinely live state possess that the current solver fails to value, preserve, represent, or reconstruct?** A single deeply observed level can answer that more efficiently than another broad untargeted sweep.

The microscope is a diagnostic method only. It must not become a route for ID-specific policy, hint-guided production behavior, or repeated treatment search until one specimen happens to solve.

## Why R03351

`R03351` belongs to the frozen 28-level class-5 first-loss population and is unusually informative within it:

- required length: 119;
- ordinary width-2000 beam retains known-solution support to depth **47** before the known-live family is lost;
- `intsBucketRetention` reaches depth 46, with no solve;
- `mechanicBucketRetention` reaches only depth 34, with no solve;
- therefore the level is not merely failing at initial exposure, and two plausible diversity-retention axes have already failed to rescue it;
- its canonical accepted hint has strong provenance: the same parent-valid path was replayed successfully from many independently solved family variants, so the labelled continuation is a robust witness rather than a single opaque historical artifact.

This makes R03351 a good specimen for distinguishing a late future-feasibility signal from generic "more width" or "more diversity" stories.

## Frozen question

At the first production-faithful extinction boundary for the accepted R03351 witness, **what distinguishes the last genuinely live witness-prefix state from the states that outrank/displace it, and is that distinction representable as a generic current-level/current-search signal?**

Do not broaden the question until this one is answered or declared non-diagnostic.

## Protocol

### A. Reproduce the boundary, not the whole problem

Use the same level, accepted witness family, gate/profile/beam regime and small research observer semantics as the established first-loss work. Capture the last boundary with positive known-witness support and the first boundary with zero support. Record the exact removal cause and the on-path candidate's rank/score margin immediately before loss.

If the current branch changes the boundary materially, stop and reconcile before interpreting anything.

### B. Freeze a tiny comparison set

At the extinction depth, retain:

1. the best-ranked candidate still compatible with at least one accepted R03351 witness prefix (the **live candidate**);
2. a small fixed set of candidates that survive while the live candidate is culled: the cutoff-adjacent survivor, the highest-scored survivor, and at most two structurally distinct survivors if needed.

Do not mine hundreds of losing states for a classifier.

### C. Exact/reference adjudication

Use WS5 narrowly to answer future-feasibility questions for the frozen states/prefixes:

- does the live candidate admit at least one valid completion under the untouched level constraints? It should, but verify the exact state/prefix being compared rather than assuming witness-prefix bookkeeping is sufficient;
- do the selected outranking survivors admit any valid completion from their exact current states?
- if both sides are completable, what is the smallest future obligation/slack distinction between them?
- if the survivors are dead while the culled candidate is live, identify the earliest sound certificate or bounded lookahead signal that separates them.

This is adjudication, not a broad exact-solver acquisition program. Stop rather than expand into general CP-SAT/ZDD/etc. machinery unless the comparison itself demonstrates why such machinery is required.

### D. Compare only causally plausible, runtime-legal distinctions

Inspect differences in categories already meaningful to the solver/domain, for example:

- remaining exact-length slack and unavoidable-distance lower bounds;
- remaining intersection requirement versus attainable/revisitable intersection opportunity;
- must-cross / portal / filter obligations and their ordering compatibility;
- residual connectivity, trapped-region/interface capacity, and remaining access to required landmarks;
- parity or endpoint-feasibility constraints;
- commitment/irreversibility: whether visited geometry has consumed a future route or crossing opportunity;
- state information that is present but ignored by scoring/retention versus information not represented at all.

Historical hint identity may label the live side offline, but no candidate mechanism may use level ID, family membership, stored hints, or historical outcomes at runtime.

### E. Mechanism classification before treatment

Classify the first useful distinction before writing a treatment:

- **ranking failure:** signal already exists in state and could change score/order;
- **retention failure:** live and dead candidates need a justified multi-hypothesis retention axis materially different from the two closed bucket keys;
- **state-representation failure:** future-relevant histories collapse into the same effective representation;
- **pruning failure:** a sound/unsound prune or lower bound destroys the viable continuation;
- **reachability/operator failure:** the solver can diagnose a live continuation but current search/repair operators cannot reach it;
- **commitment/restart failure:** viable alternatives exist but are discarded through an identifiable commitment boundary.

Only the observed class may reopen its corresponding workstream/premise.

### F. Smallest generic intervention

If a causal distinction survives exact adjudication, implement the smallest level-blind intervention that expresses it. One intervention only. Preserve the ordinary work-budget contract unless the mechanism specifically demonstrates that the contract itself is the failure.

A treatment that simply recognizes `R03351`, its exact path, exact coordinates as a lookup, or its stored hint is invalid.

### G. Advancement gate

A microscope result is **positive** only if it produces one of:

1. a compact generic signal that correctly separates the frozen live candidate from the dead/less-viable comparison states and changes the diagnosed boundary in the predicted direction; or
2. a precise architectural/reachability limitation that explains why no such local signal is available and nominates a materially new solver capability.

If an intervention solves R03351, immediately test the mechanism on a small independent phenotype-matched set before any production claim. Prefer 3-5 levels first; use the remaining frozen first-loss population only after the tiny holdout shows recurrence. A one-level solve never licenses promotion.

## Stop conditions

Close or pause the microscope without further treatment search if:

- exact adjudication finds no meaningful future-feasibility difference among the frozen comparison states;
- the only separating information is hint-derived, level-specific, or unavailable to a legal cold solve;
- the distinction reduces to already-closed generic width/bucket/scorer tuning with no changed premise;
- the experiment starts accumulating feature variants or interventions without a single causal story;
- solving the specimen requires effectively encoding its answer rather than exposing a generic capability.

A negative microscope is still useful if it eliminates a plausible capability class and sharpens the next specimen/question.

## Intended outputs

The first execution should produce a small forensic report, not a new framework. It should contain:

- exact extinction boundary and live-candidate rank/margin;
- frozen comparison states;
- exact/reference completion verdicts;
- smallest observed future-feasibility distinction, or an explicit null;
- mechanism classification;
- one next action or closeout decision.
