## Contract

The preflight required a genuine closed-loop comparison: two exact-labelled prefixes had to share level, start gate, and endpoint. Portal jumps would abstain. Static obstacle components were defined from blocks, geese, and statically-dead flippers. The historical centroid puncture was replaced by every actual obstacle-cell center, with unanimous component winding required.

The source population was the resolved B2 exact-prefix map already consumed by the September future-feasibility rejoin. No new exact labels were bought and no solver behavior changed.

## Result

The census found:

- **28** exact-labelled prefixes;
- **14** levels;
- **26** fixed-endpoint groups;
- **0** fixed-endpoint groups containing both LIVE and DEAD;
- therefore **0** LIVE/DEAD contrasts eligible for winding;
- 0 portal exclusions and 0 puncture-instability cases, because no contrast reached those gates.

The preregistered advancement condition required portal-free fixed-endpoint LIVE/DEAD contrasts on at least two independent levels and robust nonzero winding differences on at least two levels. It fails before topology is evaluated.

## Interpretation

This does **not** say LIVE and DEAD frontier histories occupy the same homotopy class. The exact-labelled population simply does not place opposite labels at common endpoints.

That distinction matters because closing paths with an arbitrary straight segment, or any endpoint-dependent connector chosen after seeing this coverage, would turn connector geometry into part of the measured winding. The correct response is not to relax rigor until a comparison appears.

A possible successor is an open-path H-signature defined relative to a fixed reference system chosen independently of exact labels. Such a signature must have a clear invariance argument and controls showing it carries information beyond endpoint identity before it can become decision-bearing.
