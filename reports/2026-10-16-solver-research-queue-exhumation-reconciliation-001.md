## Durable process change

The operating model now carries a **queue completeness / no-stranding invariant**. After a material result or apparent queue exhaustion, reconcile workstreams, future-work, archaeology, plans/reports, the premise overlay and the question graph. An executable unresolved descendant moves into workstreams; a non-executable descendant keeps a precise blocker.

This is meant to prevent another false-empty queue caused by research state living in the wrong document.
