## Synthesis candidates that emerged

`S2-C01` through `S2-C13` are local Phase-2 handles only. They are not canonical premise IDs.

The main synthesis families are:

- **Evidence authority as carried context.** Population, participation/dose, independent unit, work contract, causal method, architecture epoch, instrumentation, maturity and permitted consumers determine what a measurement is allowed to mean downstream.
- **Residuals as portfolio-conditioned negative images.** Current residual prevalence is often evidence about complementarity under the predecessor portfolio, not automatically a general Pathfinder property.
- **Constructor + observer as an evidence-generation pipeline.** Which states become visible can change when the constructor changes even if the observer does not.
- **Independent unit as a decision-bearing choice.** Row count cannot substitute for parent/state/trajectory/causal-unit replication.
- **A recurring observation-to-decision seam.** Multiple technically unrelated bounded positives stall at producer -> authority -> lifetime -> consumer -> counterfactual-action/economics rather than at phenomenon detection itself.
- **Non-puzzle decision state.** Remaining work, uncertainty, provenance, failed attempts, retained-set context, causal commitments, artifact age and authority may matter even when geometric puzzle state is unchanged.
- **A missing middle consumer experiment.** Counterfactual decision replay or an equivalent bounded consumer test can sometimes sit between `phenomenon exists` and `build a production treatment`, with live matched-work experiments still required where feedback invalidates replay.
- **Information value distinct from immediate path progress.** A probe or abandoned action may have value only through a downstream decision or retained artifact; runtime value remains unproved.
- **Generation-versus-selection separation.** Selector diagnoses require proof that the useful continuation was generated at the same semantic granularity; generator proposals require evidence that downstream selection would retain/use their output.
- **Producer-consumer and lifecycle interfaces.** Derived facts need an explicit consumer class, and action lifetime need not equal artifact lifetime.

## The live-state anomaly

The planned anomaly is real in the current repository: Lane A structural decomposition, D1 future-intersection realizability, D2 constrained-event signal, D3 legal interface commutativity, F3 topology signal, and bounded H3 allocation evidence all contain some positive phenomenon without earning a production treatment.

Phase 2 did **not** conclude that one solver mechanism explains them. The common surviving hypothesis is narrower: several results reach observation but lack a demonstrated, economical and properly-authorized decision contract. The smallest test of that hypothesis is a current decision-point/consumer census plus producer-consumer contracts for technically unrelated positives. If those positives require unrelated new architectures, the unification should be retired.

## Missing evidence and experimental primitives

Phase 2 identified several small primitives that could discriminate among rivals without prematurely implementing solver treatments:

- a read-only evidence-contract record carrying population, work contract, participation/dose, causal method, epoch, authority/maturity and permitted consumers;
- residual provenance/enrichment joins against predecessor capabilities;
- population-construction manifests including constructor identity, admissibility, censoring/abstention, sampling policy and independent-unit structure;
- explicit independent-unit metadata;
- producer/consumer contract templates;
- a current decision-point state and consumer census;
- bounded counterfactual decision replay where feedback permits it;
- a cheapest-consumer economics envelope covering eligibility, production cost, disagreement, displaced-work bounds, soundness and churn;
- candidate-source plus witness-relative first-loss evidence for generation-versus-selection diagnosis;
- artifact-lifecycle telemetry for knowledge produced by abandoned actions.

These are hypotheses about missing research capability. Phase 2 does not authorize their implementation.

## Historical/epoch qualification

Historical research was checked through the rename-aware archaeology rather than only current vocabulary. Known lineages include `archetype -> routing regime`, compact attempt keys -> canonical structured attempt identities, `diverse beam -> mechanic-bucket retention`, `dedup near-tie -> coarse-state near-tie retention`, `atlas/oracle -> labelled branch/reference model`, `trove -> variant family dataset`, and `random/randoms -> corpus2`.

Historical apparent negatives with option/result transport failures, disabled participation, bundled treatments, changed residuals, or different work contracts are therefore retained only at their earned scope. Old naming or a reverted implementation is not used as evidence that the broader premise is true or false.
