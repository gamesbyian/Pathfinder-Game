## Question

Do the selected symmetry solve/work cliffs collapse when the arbitrary coordinate-dependent dimensions identified by the microscope are controlled?

Two factors are separated:

1. **equivariant candidate order**: a research-only hook orders each symmetry sibling's legal candidates by the corresponding direction in the parent coordinate frame. It changes no legality, score, slack, prune or budget.
2. **fixed repair seed**: the existing research-only repair seed hook gives a transformed gate the same deterministic PRNG identity as its inverse-mapped parent gate.

DFS and no-tie admissible-order have no seed factor, so they run control vs equivariant-order only. Repair runs control, order-only, fixed-seed-only and combined.

## Causal interpretation

For DFS/admissible-order, the parent remains ordinary production ordering. The transformed sibling's order arm is expressed in that same parent frame. If the ordering seam is sufficient for a selected cliff, the transformed arm should converge toward the parent control in solve state and, where both solve, work.

For repair, the four variant arms distinguish:
- control: current ordering + coordinate-derived seed;
- order: parent-frame ordering + coordinate-derived seed;
- seed: current ordering + parent-mapped seed;
- combined: parent-frame ordering + parent-mapped seed.

The strongest attribution would be combined convergence where neither single factor suffices. A single-factor rescue/normalization instead attributes the selected cliff primarily to that factor. No change is also informative and sends the microscope to the next unmeasured non-equivariant seam.
