## Cancellation is scoped to economic priority, not soundness

After this frozen test was authored, the source-exact native beam lineage experiment reproduced both R02182/R02801 nominated prefix identities and measured only **216** and **2** units of actual gross descendant work under the original beam. The prespecified ten-thousand-work screening threshold failed for both; no consumer, cache or hard prune was promoted. The published four-parent whole-suffix graph inclusion remains **20/20 positive**. Its route-graph shortest-path implementation has not been independently tested on these twenty controls, so do not misinterpret cancellation as evidence of DP correctness.

The precommit below preserves the precise method and controls. It may be reactivated only after a **different** production-source candidate establishes material downstream opportunity, a cheap trigger and a reason to pay the derivation cost. Keeping an active correctness PR for a failed selected consumer would misrepresent the programme's execution priority.

## Why another check is not redundant

The passed first-hint four-parent LIVE audit **traced real move sequences** into the shadow successor graph. That established inclusion of accepted continuations, but not correctness of the **terminal distance computation and group-order dynamic program** used to assert DEAD candidates on the generated corpus. A bug in Dijkstra seed encoding, pair-used transitions, path segmentation, or terminal arrival orientation could incorrectly return no route even while the graph contains an actual accepted solution. This new check tests that specific missing implication, not another random opponent set.

Use the same four accepted first published hints and fixed prefix depths, normalized raw mechanics, and selected two still-required MP sites, one zero-prior-visit MC, one fully unused portal pair. Run source-native prefix replay and the **same four-terminal metric code** used for the generated #2213 experiment, with a cap of 5000 settled Dijkstra states per seed. When an accepted source suffix exists, a non-censored necessary minimum **greater than** counted remaining, or unreachable, is a decisive correctness alarm; leave all production prune paths disabled. A cap-abstention is UNKNOWN, never a correctness success or failure. A positive test means no such violation across qualifying published-source parent controls, but does not establish universal soundness by testing alone.

Precommitted success: 4/4 published official first hints referee accepted, at least 2 distinct source parent IDs with noncensored applicable metric results, and **zero** noncensored false exclusion of the accepted full path. Source effects are read-only. Collect exact member SHA, per-parent counts, min/max abstract pops/arcs, and missing-cap denominators. If the result is negative, preserve the explicit original LIVE counterexample and stop promotion instead of post hoc tuning. The economic/descendant gate remains with #2214 and generated-LIVE soundness remains a separate requirement.
