## Decision-bearing findings

The census materially changed how the solver's earlier “budget starvation” telemetry should be interpreted.

- The isolated-technique oracle union solves **246/879 (28.0%)** currently-unsolved levels when each technique receives its own full 50,000,000-node budget.
- Plain `dfs:repair:repair` solves **119/879 (13.5%)**, but still reaches the node cap on **750/879 (85.3%)**. This closes the broad hypothesis that repair mainly fails because the shared ladder does not give it enough of the same search.
- The three repair-family rows account for **76 unique solves** in total (47 plain + 17 turn-biased + 12 must-turn-biased), while the five `ida:*` rows account for only **6 unique solves** in total. This closes the proposed density-conditioned admissible-order reserve as too thin a capability edge to justify production routing.
- The oracle union also exposed a different and much more actionable failure class: many isolated wins are extremely cheap but their winning technique is not generated by the production attempt policy for that level. That finding led directly to the current beam-routing and late bounded-repair work tracked in the live queue.

These are population observations about isolated technique capability. They do **not** authorize production policy to use exact level identity or stored winning configurations. Production routing must remain level-blind and mechanics/current-state-derived.
