The two negative arms are **not interchangeable**. The 911 suffix tests merely reject full completions built from already-known solution tails, and do not prove that any individual revised prefix is DEAD. The 12 CP-SAT **INFEASIBLE** decisions are actual independent complete-future impossibility results for those twelve specific, genuinely move-legal revised prefixes. They do not label the remaining 80 move-legal proposals. Parent independence is 12; neither 92 variants nor 911 attempts are independent trial counts.

The prototype has no cold input: proposed substitutions are copied from stored hint paths in the same puzzle. That makes the result an offline mechanism falsifier only, and would make any future solve from this producer **hint-assisted**, not cold. The fresh original-fate labels and the subsequent exact revised-fate labels were never used to select any runtime solver policy. The main solver's action allocation, work meter, pruning, repair and ranking remained unchanged.

## Stop decision and surviving question

The literal form `historical solution segment + identical two endpoints + identical raw edit length + retain substantial dead prefix head/tail + historical hint continuation` was well exposed (796 proposals, 92 legal) but recovered no exact-LIVE outcome. More CP-SAT spending on the remaining 80 geometrically similar patched prefixes is **not warranted** without a stronger, current-state causal discriminator; the twelve-parent negative is a proportional discovery stop, not a theorem that no one of the 80 can work.

The broader PG-K concept is **not** closed merely because one source of patches did not restore feasibility. A novel successor must obtain a bounded genuinely choice-bearing failure/commitment interface from *current* search, not memorized winning hints, and show why the edit can repair a coupled mechanical constraint that equal-length swaps did not. Plausible alternatives include length-changing revisions, parent-labeled obligation changes, or earlier causal rollback that preserves unaffected prefix segments. PG-P's read-only dependency-descent work is a cheap precursor; new repair operators and broader oracle sampling must be earned by explicit opportunity economics.

No new cold solve, production code, treatment ablation, allocation budget or hint record was created. Temporary Actions workflows used to capture this result must be removed prior to integration. The reproducible maintained script remains `scripts/stress/pg-k-dead-prefix-commuting-revision.mjs`.
