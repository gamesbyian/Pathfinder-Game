## Why a new experiment is justified

The September `WS6` observer in [WS6 001](2026-09-20-ws6-dependency-interface-observer-result-001.md) already compared **post-divergence** current-state facts for four exact LIVE/DEAD pairs under one parent and found path-resource and must-pass-bound differences. The subsequent [independent-parent attempt](2026-10-01-ws6-independent-parent-replication-result-001.md) had 2/24 eligible independent parents under an expensive and mostly abstaining CP-SAT contract. Rerunning that same snapshot observer is not a new question or an acquisition.

The unresolved [descent-shadow archaeology](2026-09-13-solver-archaeology-repair-descent-shadow-orphan-016.md) asks whether real repair trajectories commit to coupled mechanics **during the descent** toward a near miss, not merely at the eventually-identified DEAD/LIVE branch. Current repair provides source-native `_repairChoiceResearchObserver` and `_repairEliteResearchObserver` read-only hooks. This pilot collects those existing events as part of the unmodified 20k-node repair search, then reconstructs a bounded structural event history on each of up to four unique lowest-badness elites.

It reports chosen modes and branching exposure, elite acquisition node ordinal, same-badness but early-divergent elite counts, and retrospective alignment with the closest accepted solution by shared raw prefix. Event markers are coarse: portal jumps, revisit, turn, must-pass/cross/turn cell visits and visit occupancy; these are **not** full future-complete mechanic states. No hint steers a solver step.

## Stop and continuation

A retrospective shared-prefix contrast is at best descriptive. To merit further work, evidence must identify a repeatable **pre-terminal** commitment-family pattern under the actual research hooks on multiple independent parents, beyond simple different coordinates and visited geometry, with a plausible inexpensive *current-state* consumer to test. Same-badness elites with unrelated early paths may show basin heterogeneity; they are not inherently causal or repairable. If all differences reduce to first raw move mismatch, no correlated stage identity, or no before-terminal observation, conclude negatively for this observer form.

Do not buy CP-SAT merely because a local event tag differs. Do not promote any repair parameter. One-shot workflow is disposable; preserve source SHAs, full machine result, source identities and experiment limitations, then integrate the live queue and question as warranted.
