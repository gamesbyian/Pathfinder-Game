## What has actually been ruled out

A source-matched 250k isolated method pass finds no success at all on these twelve parents under its exact five-method choice. This closes the *tested form* “simply select one of these five methods and give it 250k work” for these twelve sample parents. It does **not** rule out a shared/incremental method mix or a different mechanism that reaches the same fruitful branch earlier. It is not a proof that all possible 250k cold solvers fail.

The two 10M-recovered parents are useful selected-on developmental microscopes, particularly `R03031` where two differently retained beam variants find different valid paths. The appropriate next signal would be **where** the two beam runs first diverge, whether they rejoin useful basin states, and what share of 2.2–3.1M work is irreducible discovery versus duplicate/misallocated work. An unprincipled width/profile/seed sweep over these two parents would be selection-on-outcome exploitation, not independent cold gain evidence. A current-input-only consumer must be tested at shared budget and on independent parents before promotion.

The ten remaining parents show **measured** five-method non-success at 10M on this source, unlike the old no-recorded-success projection. Any deeper causal experiment should preselect a tiny subset of those parents and use witness-preserving controlled variants to test fragility/robustness, with no bulk family-generation authorization.
