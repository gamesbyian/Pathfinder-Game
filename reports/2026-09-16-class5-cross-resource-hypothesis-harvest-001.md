## Why this pass

The current Class-5 frontier is 390 production misses with no known admissible/T1 candidate. Existing work has already closed several tempting nearby forms: generic scorer tuning, six simple future-feasibility summaries, connectivity/dead-cause reuse in the tested population, low-cardinality beam quota keys, fixed-endpoint homotopy, broad restart retuning, and generic positional repair.

The useful question is therefore not "which old idea has not been retried lately?" It is: **what kind of information does the solver repeatedly appear not to represent, preserve, or exchange?**

This pass rejoins exact labels, prefix survival, repair exposure/reachability, accepted-path/variant evidence, lifecycle/work evidence, and archaeology. The three cards below are deliberately relational. None is another scalar feature sweep.

## Cross-action causal matrix

Use this as a classification lens, not a new algorithm:

| Search fact | Likely missing capability |
|---|---|
| useful state is never generated, but several actions can finish when seeded there | state-generation / future-feasibility reasoning |
| one action generates a useful state, another can finish it, but the second never reaches it | exposure / continuation / handoff (Card E) |
| useful material is generated but repeatedly culled | retention / state representation |
| action reaches the right region but all completions fail | operator/search capability |
| extra work repeatedly revisits the same structural response family | marginal-information / allocation problem |

This prevents "unsolved" from collapsing generation, exposure, retention, and completion into one failure label.

## H1 — residual event-feasibility / completion-regime structure

### Premise

The solver may represent **how much** obligation remains more faithfully than **which combinations and orders of future events remain jointly realizable**.

The September exact-labelled future-feasibility pass is important negative evidence here: 31 exact-labelled states across 15 levels showed extensive LIVE/DEAD overlap for all six prespecified scalar summaries (`intersectionCapacitySlack`, `freeIntersectionSlackAfterMustCross`, `pendingLowDegree2`, `minPendingDegree`, `activeJointClusters`, `jointRejects`). That closes nearby scalar scarcity/count formulations, not the categorical feasibility question.

Other evidence points in the same direction:

- exact extinction work establishes cases where current rank prefers exact-DEAD material over an exact-LIVE sibling;
- the promoted must-cross x consumed-portal-terminal prune is a concrete example where two individually ordinary facts become decisive only through their **joint future consequence**;
- older future-intersection / blueprint work asked whether future crossing commitments remained realizable, but production formulations were dirty or silently disabled rather than cleanly falsifying the premise;
- accepted-path and mechanic-bucket work repeatedly shows that obligation/mechanic timing and ordering can matter without being reducible to monotone urgency.

### Smallest falsifying observer

On a frozen set of exact-labelled LIVE/DEAD sibling states, derive a small, prespecified set of **events** from the state itself, such as:

- satisfy a pending must-cross axis;
- realize one required future intersection through a nominated local region/interface;
- traverse an unused portal family/terminal where relevant;
- visit a pending must-pass / landmark through a particular remaining interface;
- preserve one of two observed obligation-order alternatives from accepted paths.

Use offline exact/reference machinery only to ask relational questions:

1. Is each event individually realizable from the state?
2. For selected event pairs, are both jointly realizable?
3. Where accepted-path evidence nominates an ordering question, is A-before-B realizable, B-before-A realizable, both, or neither?

Do **not** enumerate arbitrary cells or all event permutations. The event set must be nominated by the state/accepted-path/exact-loss question before labels are inspected.

### Advancement bar

Advance only if a compact categorical relation recurs across unrelated parents, for example LIVE siblings preserve a joint event/order regime that DEAD siblings systematically lack while scalar progress/slack remains overlapping.

A positive result earns a reusable offline event-feasibility microscope or a narrowly justified runtime abstraction. It does not directly earn intersection blueprints, synthetic must-cross anchors, or a broad alternate solver.

A null result closes this bounded relational formulation and pushes acquisition toward a more expressive search mechanism rather than more feature accretion.

## H2 — marginal information velocity / structural stasis

### Premise

Canonical work can continue increasing after a search action has stopped buying materially new structural information.

Historical evidence is recurrent but was never turned into this observer question:

- July repair diagnostics found levels that rapidly converged to a near miss and then reproduced the same length + pending-must-turn deficit family across tens of thousands of fresh restarts and many stagnation bursts;
- August equal-work restart/continuation effects were regime-dependent: null at 16M, positive on the selected <=6 near-miss band at 64M, then null on a different 7-9 band at 150M;
- September first-loss repair exposure was strongly dose-insensitive: a 10x work increase bought no exposure improvement on 26/28 levels.

These do not justify another restart treatment. They nominate an observer of **marginal search novelty**.

### Smallest falsifying observer

For one or two current expensive stages, at prespecified canonical-`workSpent` checkpoints record compact, legal current-solve summaries of newly reached search structure. Candidate summary dimensions must reuse existing state/telemetry where possible and may include:

- first occurrence of a new residual obligation/deficit signature;
- new mechanic-progress / obligation-order signature;
- new best badness or deeper known-prefix support in offline-labelled studies;
- new canonical state-family / branch-decision signature at a bounded sample rate;
- whether the next work tranche produces any new best/frontier class or solve.

The quantity of interest is not raw unique-state count. It is **new decision-relevant structural response per unit of additional `workSpent`**, joined to marginal outcome.

### Advancement bar

Advance only if a compact legal stasis signal predicts near-zero marginal continuation value beyond generic difficulty/depth/work controls on independent rows.

A positive result can earn an allocation/continuation premise. It does not authorize generic restart, early termination, bandits, or dynamic scheduling without a matched-work counterfactual.

## H3 — dependency-defined repair commitment interface

### Premise

Repair locality may be governed by a small set of coupled earlier commitments rather than rollback distance or prefix length.

Evidence already says path distance is a poor abstraction:

- generic positional/prefix repair was negative under shared work;
- exact repair-retreat work found some elites needing deep apparent rollback while others were feasible after only 1-2 rollback steps despite 27-29-step known-solution divergence proxies;
- on the September first-loss frontier, seeded repair could complete 4/28 exact live beam-cull states while 24/28 exhausted the diagnostic ceiling; this population was not unusually operator-incapable relative to the broader repair program, so raw incapability rate itself is not the new premise.

The unresolved question is **which earlier commitments make a later continuation repairable or irreparable**.

### Smallest falsifying observer

On already exact-labelled repair-retreat or seeded-reachability cases, compare a dead elite/current continuation with a rescuing feasible continuation and record only future-relevant commitment differences, for example:

- must-turn arrival/exit orientation;
- must-cross axis already committed/consumed;
- portal-use identity/history;
- intersection placement/order commitments;
- region/interface entry consumption;
- obligation visitation order;
- path-history facts that make a later opportunity unavailable.

Ask whether a substantially smaller commitment set than positional rollback distance recurs across unrelated levels and whether reconstructable vs non-reconstructable cases differ in that interface.

### Advancement bar

Advance only if a compact dependency interface recurs and predicts the operative repair boundary. Then WS6 may earn a dependency-conditioned neighbourhood or descent-aware observer. Do not jump to CP-LNS, adaptive destroy severity, or new operators from descriptive differences alone.
