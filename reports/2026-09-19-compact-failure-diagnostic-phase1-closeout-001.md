> **Status:** concluded-positive
> **Last evidence:** 2026-09-19 — representative canary `35423841173` plus the maintained-producer suitability audit.
> **Decision:** Phase 1 is complete with a conservative disposition: the prune/beam-flow/progress bundle remains supported **research-only opt-in** telemetry; no maintained workflow enables it durably by default yet.
> **Remaining gate:** a scoped producer may be promoted later only after a real consumer demonstrates incremental decision value beyond automatic compact attempt response under that producer's own population/control semantics.
> **Evidence role:** infrastructure disposition; no solver efficacy claim.

## What is settled

The representative canary already established for the compact observer bundle:

- exact solve/status/solution/node/work parity;
- about 0.46% aggregate hosted wall overhead vs observer-off;
- about 35.7 KB compact payload across the 16-parent sample;
- real beam, DFS and repair progress-family coverage.

An equivalent generic calibration rerun is not required.

The producer audit then established that "standard compact producer" does not mean one universal scientific role. Maintained workflows span:

- whole-corpus production-ladder refresh;
- selected production-ladder targets;
- paired A/B samples;
- fresh residual confirmation;
- unsolved-only high-budget sweeps;
- isolated method/census searches;
- fixed-work portfolios;
- history-aware replay;
- specialist variant-family/diagnostic rails.

Universal default enablement would therefore pay cost and create interpretation surface in workflows with no demonstrated recurring consumer.

## Durable disposition

### Automatic compact attempt response

Remains the broad durable default layer.

It is the first evidence rung.

### Compact prune/flow/progress diagnostics

Remain:

`research-only opt-in / canonical semantics / parity-calibrated`

They may be enabled by a precommitted investigation without another infrastructure-promotion ceremony.

They are **not** silently enabled across all maintained producers.

### Rich search-loss capsules

Remain selective research capture and are unaffected by this disposition.

## Future scoped-promotion candidates

If recurring incremental value is demonstrated, the best candidates are:

2. `solver-level-blind-targeted-sweep.yml` for bounded residual/diagnostic acquisition;

This list is a scope nomination, not authorization.

Other producers may be considered if their own consumer and control semantics justify the telemetry.

## Incremental-value test

A future scoped promotion should answer:

> Does this diagnostic bundle repeatedly change the next research action compared with the automatic compact attempt response alone?

Examples of a positive answer:

- automatic response says exposure is comparable, while typed prune evidence consistently routes the question to one rejection counterfactual;
- automatic response is ambiguous, while flow/progress repeatedly distinguishes retention loss from budget censoring;
- the same compact diagnostic is consumed across recurring current-boundary analyses without bespoke reruns.

Examples of a negative answer:

- diagnostics merely restate node/work censoring already present in automatic response;
- the producer has no solved controls needed by the intended consumer;
- the observer fields are collected but never alter which experiment is run next.

## Consequence for the integration plan

Phase 2 and later consumers do not wait for another Phase-1 decision.

They use automatic compact response first and explicitly opt into compact diagnostics only when their preflight requires the extra information.

That is the final Phase-1 disposition unless a future scoped promotion earns a changed contract.
