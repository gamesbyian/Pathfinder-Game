## Newly earned next steps

1. **Repair singleton decline (59→50) deserves a one-line watch, not action.** A ~15% drop in repair's own exclusive-win count without a corresponding drop in its overall `solvedLevels` count (787→770, a much smaller ~2% move) suggests some of repair's former singleton wins are now shared with another technique that gained coverage — consistent with the 81-level "production misses gained isolated support" delta already documented, not evidence of a repair regression. No action; flag for the next refresh to see if the trend continues.
2. **The width-inversion mechanism (row 15) needs a fresh explanation, not the old one.** The 2K/5K objective-beam inversion itself is real and reproducible across both syntheses, but its explanatory feature set flipped entirely. If this inversion becomes decision-relevant to a scheduler change, do not reuse the September-1 "smaller beam's wins are larger-scale" framing — it no longer holds.
3. **The missing-exposure priority (row 18) needs re-weighing against search-quality work, not automatic re-prioritization.** September 1's "prioritize one cheap missing-exposure beam pilot before richer selector machinery" recommendation was built on missing-exposure being the majority (52.5%) of the comparable residual. It is now the minority (36.9%). This does not mean exposure work is worthless — `repair|score=repair|guidance=turn-biased` is a concrete, real, currently-unoffered menu gap (13 levels) — but a future session revisiting Workstream 1/8 priority should weigh it against the now-larger offered-but-fails bucket rather than treating exposure as automatically the leading candidate.
4. **Multi-portal enrichment (row 12) is worth one more look, not a routing rule yet.** Moving from "neutral" to "modest real enrichment" (1.03x→1.10x) is directionally consistent with the strengthened diverse-beam portal signal (rows 13-14) — together they are a slightly more coherent portal-sensitivity story than September 1 had, but still below the bar this repo's own workstream rules set for a routing intervention.

## Caveats

Every row above compares one snapshot pair; neither is a controlled confirmation of the other, and both are development/observational evidence per this repo's evidence-role taxonomy. Selection/multiple-comparison risk is real across 17+ scored rows — treat "reversed"/"strengthened" verdicts on any single row as a nomination for a materially different explanation, not as independently confirmed causal claims.
