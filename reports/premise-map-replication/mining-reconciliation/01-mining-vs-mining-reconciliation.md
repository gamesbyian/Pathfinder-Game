## Scope

This reconciliation asks only whether changing the **prospector** while holding canonical v1 fixed changes what higher-order insight emerges.

It does not use the independent peer-map discrepancy set and does not yet decide whether any finding should alter a canonical map or solver queue.

## Independence caveat

The shadow miner did not inspect the original mining definitions or results, but it did read frozen hardening files that exposed the **names** of predeclared mining lenses before its own method freeze.

The shadow branch records this as real methodological contamination.

The caveat is bounded:

- it saw names, not the dedicated preregistration definitions;
- it did not inspect original mining outputs;
- it froze a distinct six-pass method before substantive mining;
- its passes include transformations not equivalent to M1-M12, notably failure-form compression, verdict reversal, conjunctive consequence mining, relation-grammar stress, boundary inversion and generational encoding drift.

Therefore the shadow result is credible as an independently executed mining lineage, but not as a zero-exposure method-generation experiment.

This gives a real method contrast. The original miner is more explicitly **gap/interface/ontology-oriented**. The shadow miner is more **transformational/compositional/representation-historical**.

## Strong semantic rediscoveries

### 1. Non-puzzle decision state

Shadow S1 concludes that decision-equivalent state includes knowledge, failed-work history, remaining policy, continuation rights and budget.

Original Phase 2 independently produced S2-C06:

> missing state is often non-puzzle decision state

and its closeout names remaining work, uncertainty, provenance, failed attempts, retained-set context, causal commitments, artifact age and authority.

This is very strong mining-method convergence.

The formulations differ slightly:

- original mining frames the issue as a possible missing state/consumer interface;
- shadow mining frames it as a more general condition on equivalence, dominance, restart, routing and continuation.

The underlying insight is the same conceptual region.

### 2. Evidence is endogenous to its production process

Shadow S2 says search policy, portfolio order, stopping, generator grammar, trace budget and prior capability determine which evidence becomes observable.

Original mining separately synthesized:

- S2-C02 residual as predecessor-portfolio negative image;
- S2-C03 constructor + observer as evidence-generation pipeline;
- S2-C04 independent unit as decision-bearing;
- explicit residual provenance and population-construction concerns.

This is perhaps the strongest independent rediscovery in the experiment.

The shadow method compresses several original candidates into one parent: **the observation process is endogenous**.

### 3. Capability, participation, usefulness, economics and promotion are distinct

Shadow S3 separates:

- capability existence;
- participation;
- usefulness under work;
- portfolio displacement;
- composition;
- promotion.

Original mining repeatedly surfaced the same structure through:

- authority/maturity lenses;
- P198;
- S2-C01 evidence authority;
- S2-C11 cheapest-consumer economics envelope;
- Phase-3 admissions and evidence-contract logic.

This is strong convergence, though the frozen map itself already makes maturity unusually explicit, so both miners benefited from a representation that supports this distinction.

### 4. Information value/lifetime/authority are not one variable

Shadow S5 separates information lifetime from information authority.

Original mining produced:

- S2-C01 evidence authority as carried context;
- S2-C10 explicit producer/consumer class;
- S2-C13 action lifetime versus artifact lifetime;
- interface-loss results in M6/M10.

The exact shadow statement is cleaner and more compressed, but the semantic content strongly overlaps.

### 5. Option preservation differs from immediate scalar progress

Shadow S4 emphasizes preservation of future decision rights and rare completion regimes.

Original Phase 1 already surfaced preference-versus-option-preservation through M7/M10/M11, and Phase 2 separately investigated information-valued actions.

This is strong convergence at the parent level.

### 6. Historical/architectural conditioning of conclusions

Shadow boundary inversion and verdict reversal repeatedly find that interpretation changes across population, work, architecture and policy boundaries.

Original mining's M1/M8 and Phase-2 evidence-authority synthesis do the same from a different direction.

This region is robust to interrogation method.

## Same territory, materially different organization

### Original: many interface candidates
### Shadow: one decision-context-loss parent

Original Phase 2 preserves several separate candidate handles:

- missing non-puzzle state;
- producer/consumer gaps;
- artifact lifetime;
- observation-to-decision contracts;
- counterfactual consumer tests;
- information-valued actions.

Shadow mining compresses much of this into:

> capability is lost when distinctions that matter to future decisions are erased, merged, censored, or stripped of usable authority before those decisions occur.

This is not a simple duplicate. It is an alternative conceptual organization of largely overlapping evidence.

### Original: residual/observer/independent-unit distinctions
### Shadow: endogenous-observation parent

The original lineage keeps residual formation, constructor effects, observer effects and independent-unit choice as separate synthesis candidates.

The shadow lineage groups them under the deeper claim that the research process selects the evidence it later interprets.

Again, the difference is primarily abstraction level.

## Shadow-mining-only or materially stronger findings

### 1. The map is a layered semantic record rather than a homogeneous graph/table

Shadow S6 is the clearest result that does not appear equivalently in the original synthesis.

It is supported by:

- changing schemas across proposition generations;
- changing relation vocabularies;
- mixed proposition/prose endpoints;
- status labels carrying different semantic roles;
- unsafe graph transitivity;
- malformed standard-CSV field counts in four extension rows.

The original mining did challenge ontology quality and graph structure, but it did not synthesize this strong representation-history warning in the same way.

This is a genuine gain from the shadow method, especially its relation-grammar and generational-drift passes.

### 2. Verdict reversal as a first-class property of a research conclusion

Original mining has stale/epoch-sensitive authority and reopen semantics scattered across hardening and evidence-state reasoning.

The shadow verdict-reversal audit more directly asks:

> what observation would make the practical interpretation move back?

That yields a distinct epistemic test of whether statuses are one-way.

The idea overlaps original freshness/scope work but is not organized there as strongly.

### 3. Boundary inversion as a discovery tool

The shadow method systematically looks at zero/exhausted/infinite/saturated or scope-boundary cases.

This is not a named original lens and gives several useful falsifications of naive monotonic stories:

- more persistence is always better;
- same state means same continuation value;
- more capability means more solves.

Some conclusions overlap original content, but the interrogation mechanism itself adds a useful independent route.

### 4. Non-local conjunctive mining

Original Phase 2 does synthesize across findings, but the shadow pass explicitly searches for small sets of individually ordinary premises whose conjunction creates a qualitatively new question.

This produced compact questions such as:

- restart equivalence under failed-work history;
- generator selection biasing the apparent generalization unit;
- knowledge needing separate lifetime and authority metadata.

The resulting insights often overlap original Phase-2 territory, but the route to them is meaningfully distinct.

## Original-mining-only or materially stronger findings

### 1. Observation-to-decision contract as a concrete live-state anomaly

Original Phase 2 notices that several unrelated positive research lines stall between observation and economical action, producing S2-C05 and a bounded consumer-contract hypothesis.

The shadow miner sees broad context-loss and authority/lifetime issues but does not independently reproduce this **specific cross-lane live-state anomaly**.

This appears genuinely method-sensitive.

### 2. Generation versus selection as a dedicated diagnostic split

Original S2-C09 insists that selector diagnoses require evidence that the useful continuation was generated at the same semantic granularity.

Shadow mining touches option preservation, action rights and alternative loss, but does not isolate generation-versus-selection as strongly.

This is an original-method advantage.

### 3. Missing experimental primitives

Original Phase 2 explicitly asks for the smallest research primitive that would discriminate among competing explanations and produces:

- consumer census;
- producer/consumer contracts;
- counterfactual decision replay;
- cheapest-consumer economics envelope;
- residual provenance joins;
- population-construction manifests.

The shadow program intentionally does not translate discoveries into this sort of execution scaffolding.

This is mostly a design difference rather than a conceptual disagreement.

### 4. Semantic sibling and tested-form closure

M4/M5 explicitly inspect untested siblings and repeated failed forms beneath open parents.

The shadow miner does not have an equivalent dedicated pass.

Its compression pass can group related failure forms, but it does not systematically enumerate sibling-space emptiness.

### 5. Named asymmetry search

M7 searches preregistered asymmetries such as reject/recover and choose-next/stop-current.

The shadow method may discover some of these through inversion or conjunction, but it does not systematically cover a supplied asymmetry family.

This is an original-method strength and also a source of path dependence because only named asymmetries receive guaranteed attention.

## Findings weakened by the method comparison

### Raw graph centrality

Original M2/M3/M11 can make graph position highly visible.

Shadow relation-grammar and generational-drift work shows why raw topology can be representation-sensitive:

- relation generations are heterogeneous;
- some edges terminate in prose concepts;
- relation semantics are not uniformly transitive;
- proposition schemas changed over time.

Therefore original graph-structural findings remain descriptive but should carry stronger representation-history qualification.

### Cross-lens replication count

The original lineage already warns that lens replication is not empirical independence.

The shadow result strengthens that warning: multiple canonical lenses can converge partly because they share the same ontology and explicit relation substrate.

Independent method convergence is more informative than within-method lens count.

## Findings strengthened by the method comparison

The following broad regions reappear despite substantially different interrogation mechanics:

- non-puzzle decision context;
- endogenous evidence generation;
- maturity/participation/economics separation;
- option preservation;
- authority/lifetime distinction;
- architecture/population/work conditioning.

These should be treated as **interrogation-robust conceptual regions**, while still remembering that both methods operated on the same map representation.

That last clause is critical: mining robustness is not construction robustness.

## Method-dependence classification

### Robust across interrogation

- effective decision state exceeds puzzle state;
- evidence is selected by the process that produces it;
- capability/participation/usefulness/promotion differ;
- option preservation differs from scalar immediate progress;
- information authority and lifetime are separable;
- historical conclusions require scope/architecture conditioning.

### Original-method-sensitive

- live observation-to-decision anomaly;
- generation-versus-selection diagnostic split;
- explicit experimental-primitive design;
- semantic-sibling emptiness;
- named asymmetry coverage.

### Shadow-method-sensitive

- layered/generational representation warning;
- relation-grammar fragility;
- verdict-reversal semantics;
- boundary inversion;
- explicit conjunctive consequence search.

### Representation-sensitive rather than method-robust

- raw graph-centrality implications;
- status-as-maturity interpretations;
- relation-transitivity assumptions;
- conclusions relying heavily on late explicit authority/lifetime vocabulary.

## Effect of shadow contamination caveat

The frozen hardening files exposed names of original mining lenses before shadow method freeze.

That reduces confidence in claims of complete methodological independence, especially where the shadow method could have been nudged by recognizing familiar categories such as contradiction, asymmetry, interface or ontology analysis.

However:

- the six-pass shadow scheme is not a disguised execution of M1-M12;
- its most distinctive result, S6 layered semantic representation, comes from relation grammar and generation drift rather than a corresponding original lens;
- the strongest convergences arise from combinations of compression, conjunction and boundary analysis rather than from merely repeating exposed lens labels.

The comparison is therefore still useful, but convergence should be described as **independently executed under limited prior method-label exposure**, not pristine blind methodological replication.

## Bottom line

The fixed canonical v1 map contains several conceptual structures strong enough to survive a substantial change in interrogation method.

The original method and shadow method differ most in **what they do with the structure**:

- original mining is better at turning gaps into explicit interfaces, sibling spaces, discriminators and research primitives;
- shadow mining is better at compressing distant material into higher-order parents and exposing representation-history artifacts.

The largest surprise is not wholesale disagreement. It is that the two methods recover much of the same high-level terrain but partition it differently, while each exposes a smaller class of genuinely method-specific findings.

That is evidence that the original mining results are not primarily artifacts of M1-M12, while also showing that M1-M12 do not exhaust what the frozen map can reveal.
