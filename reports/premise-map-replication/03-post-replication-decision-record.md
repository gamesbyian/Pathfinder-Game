# Post-replication decision record

Date: 2026-09-17
Status: recommendation after closed construction, mining and cross-reconciliation stages

## Decision summary

The replication program does **not** currently justify another broad mining round or the full fourth cell.

It does justify a bounded map/hardening follow-up before the premise map is used as a high-confidence generator of new queue work.

## 1. Fourth cell

Do not run it now.

The disagreements that motivated the possible fourth cell are already mostly explained by the two one-variable-at-a-time comparisons.

Reopen only if a specific future decision cannot distinguish:

- a canonical-ontology artifact;
- a mining-method artifact;
- a genuinely robust concept.

## 2. Map-version follow-up

Do not directly admit new solver premises from the replication reports.

Instead, prepare a **representation-hardening pass** for the next map version or map tooling that addresses:

- explicit representation-generation/provenance metadata;
- normalized status semantics or clearly multi-axis status;
- relation-schema generation/version semantics;
- protection against treating heterogeneous edges as one graph grammar;
- validation of canonical CSV rows under a standard parser;
- explicit distinction between archival fine-grained nodes and higher-order functional parents;
- representation of execution/evidence substrate where it carries genuine research premises.

Candidate material for semantic review, not automatic admission:

- level blindness as information-flow contract;
- population identity as experimental identity;
- process scope vs solve scope;
- research artifact vs puzzle input separation;
- concept status as multi-axis;
- verdict-reversal/reopen semantics as methodological metadata.

## 3. Mining-method follow-up

Do not replace M1-M12.

Add later mining hygiene so future rounds can distinguish:

- graph findings that rely on canonical topology;
- findings that survive abstraction/compression;
- verdicts with and without explicit reversal conditions;
- conclusions stable under boundary inversion;
- representation-history artifacts.

The shadow method is valuable as a complementary audit toolkit, especially relation-grammar stress and generational-drift analysis.

## 4. Solver-queue handoff

Do not create solver queue items merely because a concept was robust across the replication.

Robustness establishes conceptual salience, not expected solve gain.

The strongest cross-axis regions should instead become **premise-confidence annotations** when future queue items are proposed:

- effective decision state exceeds puzzle state;
- work allocation/scheduler semantics are part of capability;
- evidence is endogenous to search/population/instrumentation;
- historical negatives require scoped interpretation.

When future queue work invokes one of these regions, the replication provides stronger justification that the premise is not an artifact of one map/mining lineage.

## 5. Specific existing live-state synthesis

Do not revive the broad common consumer-interface hypothesis merely because both maps contain information-flow concepts.

#1853 already tested that higher-order synthesis and found its broad shared pass condition failed. The replication supports the *general seam* but not that particular unification.

## 6. Recommended immediate next work

The cheapest useful next step is a bounded representation-hardening / v3-preparation audit, not more mining.

That audit should:

1. normalize/validate the canonical data substrate;
2. classify which peer-map execution/evidence concepts are truly absent versus represented indirectly;
3. mark topology-dependent mining conclusions;
4. decide whether any representation-only changes warrant a new map snapshot;
5. leave solver queue decisions for a separate premise-to-falsifier pass.

## Final disposition

- new broad mining round: **not earned**;
- full fourth cell: **not earned**;
- immediate solver queue expansion from replication: **not earned**;
- representation-hardening / map-v3 preparation: **earned**;
- selective methodological additions from shadow mining: **earned as audit hygiene**;
- future use of robust concepts as stronger premise provenance: **earned**.
