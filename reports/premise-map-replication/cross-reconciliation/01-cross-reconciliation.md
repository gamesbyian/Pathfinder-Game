# Cross-reconciliation of construction and mining dependence

Date: 2026-09-17
Stage: 4
Inputs:
- closed Stage 3A map-vs-map reconciliation (#1857);
- closed Stage 3B mining-vs-mining reconciliation (#1858).

## Purpose

This stage asks what the two independent discrepancy sets say together.

It does not compare raw blind artifacts from scratch again. It uses the already-closed reconciliations so that construction effects and mining-method effects were first estimated separately.

The target is a provenance-of-disagreement model:

1. robust to both representation and interrogation;
2. robust to representation but interrogation-sensitive;
3. representation-sensitive but recoverable once represented;
4. canonical-lineage dependent;
5. independent-construction-only conceptual material;
6. unresolved because both variables changed together.

## 1. Strongest cross-axis convergence

### A. Effective decision state exceeds puzzle state

This is the cleanest fully replicated region.

Construction axis:
- canonical v1 contains state-sufficiency, equivalence, continuation, policy-relative and work-history material;
- the peer map independently makes search state an epistemic boundary and distinguishes multiple meanings of state relatedness.

Mining axis:
- original Phase 2 produced non-puzzle decision state as S2-C06;
- shadow mining made this S1, its strongest convergent synthesis.

The common structure is:

> future decision value depends not only on puzzle geometry but on knowledge, failed-work history, remaining action rights, policy, continuation state and available work.

This survives both cartographer and prospector changes.

**Classification:** robust to representation and interrogation.

### B. Work allocation / scheduler semantics are part of capability

Construction axis:
- canonical v1 has budget, scheduler, participation, displacement and portfolio-conditioned capability families;
- the peer map independently makes allocation, work accounting and scheduler semantics central.

Mining axis:
- original mining repeatedly separates phenomenon, participation, economics and promotion;
- shadow S3 independently separates capability existence, participation, usefulness, displacement and promotion.

**Classification:** robust to representation and interrogation.

### C. Evidence is conditioned by the process that produces it

Construction axis:
- canonical v1 contains residual conditioning, instrumentation, generator/population and evidence-process premises;
- the peer map independently emphasizes experimental identity, architecture-relative negatives, causal measurement alignment and population/evidence boundaries.

Mining axis:
- original Phase 2 separates residual negative-image effects, constructor/observer conditioning and independent-unit choice;
- shadow S2 compresses these into the stronger parent that evidence is endogenously selected by search policy, portfolio order, stopping, instrumentation and generator grammar.

**Classification:** robust to representation and interrogation.

This is one of the most important cross-axis results because the two miners organize it differently yet arrive at the same causal region.

### D. Historical negatives require scoped interpretation

Construction axis:
- both maps preserve architecture/work/population/measurement dependence of negative results.

Mining axis:
- original M1/M8 and Phase-2 authority work recover the same need;
- shadow verdict-reversal and boundary-inversion passes independently expose it.

**Classification:** robust to representation and interrogation.

## 2. Mining-robust but construction-independence not cleanly testable

### Information authority, mechanism maturity, unit of generalization, information lifetime

Both mining methods recover these regions strongly.

The peer map also contains them prominently.

However canonical v1 incorporated P197-P200 from the earlier independent-reconstruction lineage before the v1 freeze.

Therefore the experiment cannot use map agreement on these four concepts as independent construction replication.

The correct conclusion is narrower:

- they are **mining-method robust on canonical v1**;
- they are central to the peer reconstruction;
- but their presence in both maps is causally entangled.

**Classification:** robust to interrogation; construction robustness unresolved by this experiment.

This is exactly the sort of distinction the replication design was intended to surface.

## 3. Shadow-only mining finding explained by construction difference

### Layered semantic record / representation-history fragility

Shadow S6 says canonical v1 is not safely treated as one homogeneous graph/table because:

- proposition schemas changed across generations;
- relation vocabularies changed;
- status semantics are heterogeneous;
- some relation endpoints are prose concepts;
- generic transitivity is unsafe;
- four extension rows have malformed standard-CSV field counts.

Original mining did not synthesize this as strongly.

The construction reconciliation independently shows:

- the peer map naturally chose a very different function-topology representation;
- the peer map explicitly records concept status as multi-axis;
- the peer map uses fewer, stronger semantic relations;
- canonical and peer maps place the abstraction boundary differently.

So S6 is not merely a clever shadow-method artifact.

The alternate construction independently supports the claim that representation choices materially shape what graph-style mining can see.

**Classification:** representation-sensitive insight recovered by an independent mining method.

### Consequence

Raw canonical graph centrality, degree and topology findings should remain descriptive but carry explicit representation-history qualification.

They are not invalid. Their apparent importance may partly be a property of canonical decomposition and relation-generation history.

## 4. Original-only mining finding supported by independent construction

### Generation versus selection

Original S2-C09 isolates a dedicated distinction:

- was the useful continuation generated?
- or was it generated and later rejected, ranked away, merged, starved or otherwise lost?

Shadow mining does not emphasize this split as a top synthesis.

Yet the peer map independently organizes research around distinct functions:

`generate -> reject -> prefer -> retain -> allocate -> select`

and explicitly asks whether hard residuals are missing alternatives or losing/starving them.

This is strong evidence that the original mining result is not merely a quirk of M1-M12.

The independent construction makes the same distinction structurally natural even though shadow mining did not elevate it.

**Classification:** robust to representation, interrogation-sensitive.

## 5. Original-only live observation-to-decision anomaly

Original Phase 2 identifies a specific current-research pattern: multiple positive phenomena stall before a demonstrated economical consumer.

Shadow mining does not independently reproduce that exact live-state anomaly.

The peer map, however, makes producer/consumer-like transitions natural through:

- derive;
- retain;
- remember;
- allocate;
- select;
- transfer;
- infer.

It also contains open questions about failure becoming an actionable posterior and what information should cross stage boundaries.

Thus the **general conceptual seam** is representation-robust.

What is not independently reproduced is the specific empirical claim that several current live research lanes instantiate one common observation-to-decision bottleneck.

#1853 later tested one version of that hypothesis and found the broad common-interface story did not meet its own pass condition.

**Classification:** general concept robust; specific live-state unification is original-lineage dependent and downstream-tested negative in its broad form.

This is a useful example of why conceptual salience and a particular higher-order synthesis must remain separate.

## 6. Shadow-only verdict-reversal framing

The shadow miner asks explicitly what evidence would reverse the practical interpretation of a conclusion.

Original mining contains stale-authority, architecture-epoch, tested-form and reopen-condition machinery but does not elevate verdict reversal as one central interrogation primitive.

The peer map independently contains:

- architecture-relative negatives;
- multi-axis status;
- does-not-close relations;
- measurement/causal-alignment premises;
- temporal validity as a cross-cutting axis.

So the shadow framing is supported by the independent construction.

**Classification:** concept robust to representation, explicit framing interrogation-sensitive.

This is a good candidate for future methodological hygiene, not necessarily a new solver premise.

## 7. Named sibling/asymmetry mining appears more lineage-dependent

Original M4/M5/M7 deliberately search:

- semantic sibling gaps;
- repeated failed forms under parents;
- named asymmetry families.

Shadow mining does not guarantee these searches.

The peer map deliberately aggregates many mechanic-specific descendants and does not preserve the same fine sibling topology.

This means findings whose importance depends heavily on:

- exactly how a semantic parent was split;
- which children were given nodes;
- which asymmetry pairs were preregistered

are among the most path-dependent outputs in the program.

That does not make them wrong. It means they should be interpreted as **canonical-ontology interrogation results**, not representation-neutral facts.

**Classification:** canonical-lineage dependent unless independently supported case-by-case.

## 8. Execution/evidence substrate is a real construction-dependent blind spot

The peer map's hostile audit materially extended its own first draft with:

- level blindness as an information-flow contract;
- population identity as experimental identity;
- parallel execution preserving logical task identity;
- process scope versus solve scope;
- research artifact versus puzzle input separation.

These are not prominent first-class nodes in canonical v1.

Shadow mining's S2/S6 conclusions about endogenous evidence and representation history make these peer-only emphases more consequential: infrastructure can determine what evidence exists and what it is allowed to mean.

The two independent tracks therefore meet here from different directions.

**Classification:** independent-construction-only emphasis with independent-mining support for its importance.

This is one of the clearest candidates for later map-v3 consideration.

## 9. Canonical fine-grained historical detail remains useful despite non-reproduction

The peer map compresses many:

- mechanic-specific forms;
- exact/reference-model distinctions;
- first-loss descendants;
- solution-space/regime questions;
- detailed tested siblings.

Shadow mining also reports some of these regions as comparatively low-yield for higher-order synthesis.

That does **not** imply they are map clutter.

The canonical representation is serving a different job: preserving exact closure scope and implementation/history provenance.

**Classification:** representation-specific detail with legitimate archival/epistemic value.

The replication program therefore argues against replacing the canonical map wholesale with the peer ontology.

## 10. Strongest evidence of path dependence

The experiment finds three distinct kinds.

### Construction path dependence

Most visible in:

- granularity;
- node-versus-axis-versus-relation choice;
- execution substrate prominence;
- graph topology;
- status representation.

### Mining path dependence

Most visible in:

- whether gaps become concrete experimental primitives;
- whether representation history becomes a first-class finding;
- whether verdict reversal or boundary cases are systematically explored;
- whether sibling/asymmetry spaces receive guaranteed attention.

### Lineage entanglement

Most visible in:

- P197-P200, where the canonical map incorporated material from the earlier independent lineage before the frozen comparison point.

This third category matters because it is neither ordinary construction dependence nor ordinary mining dependence. It is causal ancestry between the supposedly compared representations.

## 12. Does the fourth cell need to run?

A full fourth-cell exercise is **not currently earned**.

The main ambiguities that might have justified it are already substantially resolved:

- shadow S6's representation warning is corroborated by the independently different peer-map structure;
- original generation-vs-selection mining is corroborated directly by the peer map's functional topology;
- original observation-to-decision ideas have peer-map structural analogues, while the broad live-state unification has already been downstream-tested and narrowed;
- the strongest cross-axis concepts are visible directly in both maps and both mining outputs.

A full independent-map mining program would therefore mostly add symmetry rather than answer a live causal ambiguity.

### Possible future bounded trigger

A targeted fourth-cell probe could become useful if a future map-version decision hinges on whether:

- a canonical-only sibling/asymmetry finding survives translation into a coarser functional map;
- an execution-substrate peer-only concept produces genuinely new higher-order insights when mined;
- graph-topology conclusions remain stable after a normalized representation rewrite.

That should be a bounded discrepancy-specific probe, not another comprehensive mining campaign.

## 13. What the experiment now supports

The premise-map program's strongest conceptual conclusions are not merely products of one ontology or one interrogation scheme.

At the same time, the program should no longer treat:

- canonical graph structure;
- exact premise granularity;
- sibling-space emptiness;
- status labels;
- relation topology

as representation-neutral facts.

The map is both:

1. a record of the research space; and
2. a historically evolved instrument that shapes what is easy to discover from that record.

The replication program therefore increases confidence in the **broad conceptual terrain** while reducing confidence in naive readings of the map's exact topology.

That is a productive result rather than a contradiction.
