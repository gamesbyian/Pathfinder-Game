## Cold production-control strict-cap audit (instrument correction)

The first exact-source production-control workflow `39127859713` aborted on the *first* observed excess of `workSpent > 250000 + 512` before serializing any parent row. This was a **harness instrumentation flaw**: the overrun magnitude and the parent’s actual outcome were thrown away, so the original attempt cannot establish a 250k ladder failure or a 250k ladder solve. The normal solver's `strictTotalWorkBudget` option installs a whole-solve work cap, but search operations/repair seed rounds may cross a cap after an atomic unit of work; the relevant documented contract must be checked against actual work before making scientific claims.

The revised harness keeps exactly the original twelve frozen parents, solver inputs and 250k requested work/node budgets, **removes the arbitrary 512-unit abort tolerance**, records `workSpent` and signed-positive `workOvershoot` for every parent, and classifies any nonzero overrun as `OVER-BUDGET` regardless of solve status. Valid paths are still retained and referee-validated, but such a result is excluded from the 250k success/failure denominator. The exact-source join has a `CURRENT-CONTROL-OVER-BUDGET` abstention category. This change is observer-only; it does not change the underlying production scheduler, work accounting, ordering, or level selection.

After the observer is corrected, a new **source-paired** execution is needed because the original production workflow has no valid result and the independent isolated result came from a different execution commit. Do not waive source equality or silently promote over-budget results. If cap violations are frequent/material, the next gate is a narrow source-qualified explanation and an enforced-cap control on a separate solver-correctness lane before attempting fixed-250k cold economic claims.
