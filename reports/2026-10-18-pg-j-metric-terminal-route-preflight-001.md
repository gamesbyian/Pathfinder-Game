## Proposed lower-bound decomposition

The earlier joint route DP tracks 2 MP, 1 MC in both crossing directions and one use-once portal pair, but has to traverse `~5,000` fully labelled product states **per retained prefix**. Its cost is not justified on Corpus2 by the now saturated source-frontier eligibility.

Instead split residual feasibility into **four mandatory visit types**: MP0, MP1, MC-H and MC-V. MP has two possible arrival axes. MC-H and MC-V each require an ordinary entry along one distinct axis. The full legal route must encounter all four types in some order, then reach the goal. Compute shortest costs between possible terminal **position + incoming-axis** states using zero-cost forced portal edges and one-cost cardinal moves on a *source-prefix-frozen* network. Each segment retains the usage of **one currently selected portal pair**, threaded to the next segment by a single bit. The other portal pairs' reuse/visitation histories and **every new future non-MC edge consumption** are forgotten. The tiny group-mask ordering DP chooses the optimal route among these terminal-to-terminal segment relaxations.

Within each segment, prohibit ordinary moves into a direction already consumed at the destination by the immutable original prefix; prohibit turns over already consumed source axes; and force straight exit at MC. That last constraint is sound for this one-MC form: after first crossing the native `mustCrossMask` / crossing-lock forbids a turn, and after the second orthogonal entry both axes have been consumed, so native `isMoveDynamicallyValid` again forbids a turn. Portal-only arrivals are exempt, but a must-cross location is not a portal (one-grid-object schema invariant). A segment can otherwise revisit ordinary cells or portal pairs not tracked, producing **extra** artificial routes, never disallowing a real one. The selected pair is chosen via the Manhattan-nearest unvisited MP and packed-key tie break, with no outcome label in the selector.

The DP ignores incidental intermediate mandatory visits, MC double occupancy across segments, any intersection upper bound, other flipping-filter rules and history of path visits. That can only lower its estimate or generate optimistic witnesses. Every valid real suffix partitions at its selected four chronological obligation events, each of whose relaxed segments must be represented with the same selected pair-use bit. By nonnegative shortest-path optimality, the DP's minimum cost is **at most** the counted length of a true completion. Therefore a value greater than the actual counted-steps-remaining is a valid *necessary-condition* certificate **only in the declared, canonically normalized one-MC and two-outstanding-MP scope**. The source witness check independently projects two full accepted paths into the same graph step by step.
