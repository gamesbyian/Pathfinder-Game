## Conclusions that become stronger only after joining lines

### Class 5 is a genuine acquisition frontier under current evidence

The provenance-corrected T1 census, six-source capability-memory census, cheap class-1/2/3 rejoin, class-5 freshness scan and exact microscope work all point the same way. Known/currently demonstrated capability explains meaningful parts of classes 1–4 but none of the 431 class-5 rows in the bounded census. Freshness checks did not reveal a clean hidden base-T1 harvest. This makes “mine existing techniques harder” a weak first explanation for class 5.

### The recurring class-5 failure is not well explained by nearby ranking/retention features

Width-insensitive score culls, failed bucket-retention canaries, the broad scorer-vocabulary negative, R03229’s exact LIVE/DEAD mismatch, and the null six-summary future-feasibility pass jointly eliminate a large neighborhood of easy hypotheses. More width, another bucket, another hand-weight profile or another nearby scalar descriptor is now low information value unless a new mechanism-specific discriminator appears.

### The next class-5 premise should change information representation or reuse, but only after a cheap recurrence test

B1/B2 and R03229 say the solver can prefer states with no future over a known-live alternative. The scalar descriptor route says the obvious compressed summaries do not separate those states. Backlog excavation recovered the still-unanswered successor: perhaps the solver repeatedly proves the same compact reason for deadness but throws that reason away. That is materially different from score-feature accretion and can be killed cheaply by measuring recurrence before implementation.

### Tail work must be priced by marginal unique capability, not average expense

The late-repair 7→6 result lost two unique seed-7 rescues while saving work; goal-attraction fresh-work gained solves only when the tier actually received work; admissible-order confirmation looked cheap because the tier did no work at all. Together these make `participation × unique capability × displacement cost` the relevant repricing frame, not stage cost in isolation.
