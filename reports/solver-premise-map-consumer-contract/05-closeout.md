## Results by lane

### Lane A: separator/decomposition

The structural positive remains real and broad enough to matter: 121/390 current Class-5 residual levels have a non-trivial balanced narrow interface. The census nevertheless does not find a ready consumer contract. Current solver state contains the puzzle information, but the research line has not demonstrated a compact dynamic boundary representation preserving crossing history, exact-length/intersection obligations and relevant mechanic state.

Disposition: **representation gate**, not missing generic consumer architecture.

Reopen only when a bounded interface contract can be tested on a frozen legal-prefix population. Raw separator geometry does not earn ranking, pruning or decomposition machinery.

### Lane D1: future-intersection commitment realizability

D1 is the one lane that maps cleanly onto an existing decision seam: generated-child beam ranking/retention. Required puzzle state is already present/reconstructable and the counterfactual is concrete: zero realizable future-intersection commitments versus at least one could affect ranking/retention.

Authority remains **ranking-only**. No prune/forced-move semantics are earned.

The retained replication is strong within its selected cohort but has a crucial ancestry limitation: 18 exact-DEAD + 2 exact-LIVE states and all 220 pin-revisit queries come from one parent, `R03147`. They are rows/queries, not 20 independent parent replications.

The earned retained-evidence economics falsifier then stops because the artifacts do not retain the decision-bearing quantities required by the gate:

- production eligibility prevalence;
- candidate/sibling scores, actual frontier rank and cutoff/retention context needed to count current-policy disagreements;
- descendant/work ancestry needed to estimate displaced `workSpent`;
- observed information-production time/cost (only the 45-second per-query cap is retained).

Safe displaced-work lower bound is zero; a useful finite upper bound is not recoverable. Fixed replay cannot establish downstream solve/work value once ranking changes frontier composition.

Disposition: **local consumer exists; retained economics insufficient; no live prototype earned**.

Reopen only from production-inert observation on an independently selected multi-parent population that retains every eligible decision opportunity, current rank/cutoff outcome, D1 result or separately justified proxy, work ancestry, measured information cost and independent-unit provenance. That trigger does not itself authorize adding instrumentation now.

### Lane F3: topology side descriptor

The cheap descriptor remains a qualified diagnostic positive. Its apparently sound unique-closest-point subset is 8/8 but has zero decision-relevant discordance. All nine decisive-puncture discordant rows are tied; tied-case agreement is 7/9 and the cheap local tie refinement does not improve it.

Disposition: **decision-bearing opportunity/reliability gate**, not missing consumer architecture.

Reopen only on fresh independent parents if a reliable compact descriptor demonstrates non-zero decision-bearing discordance. No selector or prune consumer is currently earned.

## Common-interface hypothesis

The cross-cutting Phase-3 pass condition required at least two technically unrelated positives to map to existing decision boundaries through the same contract fields without requiring a new subsystem, leaving a concrete soundness/participation/economics question.

That condition fails.

- A stops before a compact decision representation exists.
- F3 stops before a reliable decision-bearing signal exists.
- D1 alone reaches a ready existing decision seam, then stops at economics/measurement.

The shared producer/consumer vocabulary is still valuable: it forced producer, authority, lifetime, opportunity population, counterfactual and cost to be named consistently. But that is a **research-method interface**, not evidence for a shared runtime bus, blackboard, oracle service or signal subsystem.

Rival-explanation reconciliation therefore favors a mixture of healthy research gating and local mechanism/economics gaps over one shared architecture gap. Ontology/documentation incompleteness was useful in exposing missing fields, but no code/runtime architecture follows from it.

## Premise-map disposition

No new premise ID is warranted.

The consumer-contract work applies P201-P206 rather than adding a semantic premise:

- P201 prevents D1 rows/queries from masquerading as independent parents;
- P202/P203 keep offline diagnostic evidence from silently acquiring runtime authority;
- P204 forces the common-interface story to compete with healthy-gating/local-gap explanations;
- P206 forces attention onto decision-bearing opportunity population rather than row count.

No v1 or v2 map artifact is changed on this branch. If a future genuinely novel premise emerges, it must go through the existing semantic novelty/admission process rather than being backfilled from this report.

## Procedural lessons carried forward

1. Verify repository/PR state, not report language. Phase 3's original closeout missed a real red fast-gate.
2. Treat mandatory-context budgets as design constraints. Phase 3 entered with only 21 bytes of headroom; this closeout initially produced a technically legal but unacceptable 13,784-byte authority and was compacted again to 12,399 bytes.
3. Audit compaction semantically. Earlier compaction removed live gates even though the byte count improved; reopen/no-restart constraints must survive compression.
4. Establish evidence ancestry before interpreting effect size. Rows, queries, punctures and interfaces are not automatically independent support.
5. Require a concrete selection boundary and counterfactual before calling something a consumer.
6. Missing retained fields are evidence about the next measurement gate, not permission to infer those fields from labels.
7. Fail attractive synthesis cleanly. The common-interface hypothesis did not meet its own pass bar and was not rescued by weakening the definition of consumer.
8. Respect the stop rule. The formal execution plan ends after Phase 3; descendants continue only as far as their explicit gates earn.
