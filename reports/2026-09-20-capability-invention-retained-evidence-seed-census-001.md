## HARVEST findings

### Admissible-order reserve starvation

R01154 and R03270 remain clean F7 examples: the existing admissible-order method solves in isolation above its current production reserve and within the total envelope. The missing operation is not semantic reasoning; the open question is matched-work allocation.

### Class-3 dose cohort

Of 23 parents, **20 are F7 censored-dose rows**. Their exact known rescuer ran in shared production but was cut off before its known isolated solve scale:

- repair-family per-attempt deadline dominates the cohort;
- five rows reproduce the already-known admissible-order reserve-fraction mechanism;
- none are exposure gaps.

These rows should not consume invention attention until their matched-work allocation questions are resolved.

## The three unresolved rows

R02196, R02206, and R02258 are different.

Their historical rescuer is an objective-first width-5000 beam:
- R02196: mechanic-buckets retention;
- R02206: mechanic-buckets retention;
- R02258: plain retention.

In the shared-production acquisition, the exact rescuer participates and reaches a real exhausted outcome at a dose near the historical isolated solve cost, but does not solve.

That does **not** yet mean the solver lacks a new capability. The isolated success comes from the historical technique census, while the shared-production observation is current. The smallest discriminator is therefore **freshness**, not architecture:

1. replay each exact rescuer in isolation on current head/current semantics;
2. preserve the same gate/config identity;
3. validate any candidate canonically;
4. compare solve outcome and work with the historical census expectation.

### Interpretation fork

- **Fresh isolated replay fails:** historical capability evidence is stale under current code/semantics. Reclassify the demand; do not invent a production consumer to recover a capability the current solver no longer demonstrates.
- **Fresh isolated replay succeeds:** current solver still owns the capability, but shared production suppresses it despite nominal exact-action participation. Then inspect the earliest execution/context divergence before choosing between HARVEST/EXTENSION/INVENTION.
- **Mixed 1-2/3 result:** split the cohort. Do not force one explanation across all three.

This replay is much cheaper and more discriminating than immediately instrumenting beam internals or proposing a new retention mechanism.

## R03147 extension nomination

R03147 remains a deliberately cautious EXTENSION row.

Exact matched DEAD/LIVE divergence shows:
- identical global accounting;
- both branches pass current hard prunes;
- local path-resource occupancy differs;
- relational must-pass lower bounds differ;
- the causally fatal move occurs at ordinary divergence.

This nominates a missing **decision-bearing composition/interface** between local path-history resource use and future obligation cost. It does not yet earn a new hard prune, repair operator, or classifier. Independent-parent replication of the frozen interface is the next gate.

## What this seed changes

The register is already useful because it blocks two symmetric mistakes:

1. calling every hard residual an invention problem when retained evidence says the solver already owns the needed capability but starves it;
2. continuing to tune allocation after exact-action evidence has exhausted that explanation.

The next population expansion should therefore be intentionally selected from residual regions **not already conditioned on known historical rescuer capability**, otherwise the register will simply rediscover that Class 3 is Class 3.
