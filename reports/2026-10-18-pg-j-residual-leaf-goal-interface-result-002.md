## Exact source, method and result

This **is not the authoritative Pathfinder move graph**. In particular, static geese exclusion, undirected teleport, forgetting previously used axis/edge information, flipping filters, crossing counts, dynamic path rules and obligations make it unsuitable as an exact viability oracle.

## Surviving contradiction and stopping decision

The exact-LIVE `P00137-w1-A2-B10-original` and exact-DEAD `P00137-w1-A2-B10-alternate` share **exactly the same** base, articulation=0, leaf=0 and goal distance=7 despite their legal prefix histories differing. The level contains two must-pass sites, a must-cross, two flipping filters, three portals, and geese. An exact completion outcome cannot be inferred from this derived graph morphology. The single collision is a decisive falsifier for the **proposed key**, not a proof that these geometric descriptors are incapable of any future ranking usefulness. Nor is zero mixed keys on a handful of samples proof of soundness; merely subdividing 25 prefixes to 25 unique keys would provide no useful compression.

The combined key also leaves just two repeat groups comprising four prefixes, only one group outcome-pure. This is **4/25 total repeated observations** with a *mixed opposite-fate group*, not a robust cached-state opportunity. Stop this additive scalar form before writing a cache, prune or expensive broad census. The cheapest meaningful successor would compare **actual replayable mechanic-state identities** for the P00137 pair (used segments/axes, counted length and intersections, obligations remaining, portal and flipper state) and identify a *specific necessary condition* violated by the DEAD prefix while preserving the LIVE prefix. Even a successful retrospective contrast would still require actual retained search-work headroom and separate cold-source confirmation before implementing a hard prune.
