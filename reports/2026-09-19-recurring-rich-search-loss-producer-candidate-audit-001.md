# Recurring rich search-loss producer candidate audit 001

> **Status:** concluded-negative
> **Last evidence:** 2026-09-19 — representative rich canary economics plus compact-producer suitability audit and current WS2 question state.
> **Decision:** no genuine recurring rich search-loss producer is scientifically justified yet. Do not enable rich capture on ordinary stress refresh or create a ceremonial scheduled producer merely to advance Phase 5.
> **Remaining gate:** a live repeated research consumer must require event/state identity beyond automatic compact response and compact diagnostics; when that occurs, prefer a bounded targeted-sweep research rail with explicit population and solved controls.
> **Evidence role:** Phase-5 producer-selection audit; no solver efficacy claim.

## Question

Phase 5 of `docs/solver-failure-evidence-research-integration-plan.md` requires a **genuine recurring rich producer** before the search-loss resource can receive its Resource Contract audit.

The question here is narrower:

> Does the repo already have a maintained workflow that should begin recurring rich capture now?

Answer: **no**.

## Why not ordinary stress refresh?

`solver-stress-refresh.yml` is the strongest general compact-response producer:

- whole canonical populations;
- level-blind production semantics;
- solved and failed controls;
- protocol identity;
- durable current-boundary role.

Those same properties make it a poor place to pay an unearned rich-observer tax.

The real canary measured rich capture at roughly **10.32%** hosted wall overhead versus observer-off, materially above compact's roughly 0.46%. Rich capture also increased the sample payload from about 35.7 KB compact to about 246 KB.

Nothing in the current queue requires every routine full-corpus refresh to retain event/state capsules.

Therefore:

`solver-stress-refresh: reject as universal/recurring rich default`.

A future deliberately sampled rich tranche associated with a refresh may still be valid if a live question requires it.

## Candidate ranking if recurrence becomes justified

### 1. Level-blind targeted sweep — preferred host

`.github/workflows/solver-level-blind-targeted-sweep.yml`

Advantages:

- explicit mechanically frozen level population;
- production-shaped level-blind ladder;
- artifact-only research semantics;
- exact population integrity;
- experiment contract/protocol identity;
- can include prespecified solved controls;
- bounded blast radius and cost;
- already the preferred dedicated shared-production acquisition rail for Class-3-style questions.

If a repeated WS2 question needs rich event/state identity, this is the default host to evaluate first.

The future implementation should add an explicit bounded rich-capture input/profile rather than making rich capture unconditional.

### 2. Routing-regime paired A/B — question-specific

Useful when the rich consumer is already a routing/action-policy comparison.

It has:

- sealed population;
- paired arms;
- controls;
- contract identity.

Do not turn it into a generic recurrence producer outside a routing treatment question.

### 3. Technique census — specialist only

Technique census has strong solved/unsolved controls and isolated action identity, but its search semantics are isolated-technique cells rather than the ordinary production ladder.

Use rich capture there only for an isolated-technique question whose mechanism genuinely depends on event/state identity.

### 4. Stress refresh — sampled companion only

If longitudinal production search-loss becomes important, prefer a small mechanically selected companion sample or selected-parent capture over universal rich capture on every refresh.

Any such design must retain a denominator tying the selected rich parents back to the full refresh population.

## Rejected recurrence sources

### High-budget unsolved sweep

Unsolved-only population and unusually high budget. Useful microscope, poor general recurrence/control frame.

### Residual confirmation

Phase-2 population is deliberately control-defined residual and treatment-specific. Rich observations are legitimate within that experiment but do not constitute a reusable general recurring population.

### Production replay baseline

History-aware. Its rich evidence would answer continuity/replay questions, not ordinary level-blind production-search recurrence.

### Diagnostics

No explicit experiment protocol contract today and published-level diagnostics have different lifecycle/side-effect semantics.

### Variant-family dataset

Specialist generated-family alternate rail. Family provenance should remain the controlling identity rather than masquerading as ordinary production recurrence.

## Trigger that would justify Phase 5

Phase 5 reopens only when all are true:

1. a stable live research question recurs across more than a one-off microscope;
2. automatic compact response was insufficient;
3. compact prune/flow/progress was insufficient or the question explicitly needs event/state identity;
4. the relevant population and solved-control semantics are known;
5. the rich capture profile/selectors needed by that question are already bounded and parity-safe or can be cheaply recalibrated;
6. the expected repeated consumer justifies the measured storage/runtime tax.

Examples include:

- repeated WS2 first-loss studies on successive current residual boundaries;
- a recurring retention-boundary question requiring replayable culled states;
- a longitudinal basin-recurrence program whose state identity cannot be represented by compact diagnostics.

A single exciting capsule, a one-off canary, or an implementation-plan checkbox is not a trigger.

## Resource-Contract consequence

Until the trigger above occurs:

- `search-loss-evidence` correctly remains `contract-only`;
- `recurringProducerDeclared=false` is a true description, not a defect to patch around;
- Phase 6 Resource Contract audit remains blocked by design.

When recurrence becomes real, the first candidate implementation should normally be a bounded targeted-sweep rich profile. After repeated real instances exist, execute the already-owned search-loss Phase-8 Resource Contract audit.
