## Advancement rule

Advance only if all are true:

1. at least 6/12 levels have at least 25 primary-cluster records;
2. pooled reached-set or boundary sharing exceeds pooled exact-state sharing by at least 10 percentage points;
3. the same direction holds in a majority of informative levels;
4. at least 25% of structurally recurring records belong to groups with more than one exact-state fingerprint;
5. recurrence is not almost entirely adjacent emission, as shown by separated `workPoint`s.

These thresholds are intentionally weaker than August's roughly 30-point structural-versus-exact gap. The purpose is to determine whether a real population survives, not to reproduce the old effect size exactly.

If the gate fails, close this descendant. Do not build a faster cause extractor merely because a two-level diagnostic proved one repeated cause can exist.
