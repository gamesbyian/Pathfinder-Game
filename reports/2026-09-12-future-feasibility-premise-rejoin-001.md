The completed class-5 microscope did not discover a new phenotype. `R03229` reproduces an already-established exact failure shape at a real width-2000 extinction boundary: the known culled witness future is **LIVE**, while the rank-1 retained survivor and width-cutoff survivor are **DEAD**. That is important recurrence, but recurrence alone does not license another generic scorer sweep, wider beam, bucket axis, or serial microscope.

The older B1/B2 extinction-adjacent CP-SAT work had already established the same core phenomenon on unrelated levels and, critically, had already nominated the useful next abstraction: **future opportunity / future feasibility**, not more score tuning. The August B2 follow-up contains both dead-preferred/live-alternative and live/live decision points. That gives a natural control structure for asking what legal current-state information tracks actual remaining feasibility rather than merely beam score or survival.

The current WS2 authority correctly says the next treatment must first earn a materially new mechanism-specific runtime-legal discriminator or architectural limitation. This report makes that requirement concrete without adding a new workstream or framework.

## The bounded question

At exact-labelled extinction states that already pass current hard pruning:

> **Does a small, generic, runtime-legal summary of residual feasibility separate LIVE from DEAD futures materially better than existing beam score / prune outcome, and does the same distinction recur across unrelated parents?**

The purpose is not to predict full puzzle solvability from arbitrary states. It is to discover whether the solver is systematically missing one compact piece of future information at the decision points where known-live material disappears.

## Prespecified descriptor families

Keep this deliberately small. Do not open a feature buffet.

### A. Exact-resource attainable-capacity summaries

Current search already reasons heavily about lower bounds and spent resources. The missing information may instead be an **upper** or attainable-set fact about what remains possible.

Examples worth testing only if they can be derived cheaply from current state:

- maximum additional intersections still attainable before goal/length exhaustion;
- remaining path-length capacity inside currently reachable cells/components;
- parity/congruence restrictions on attainable remaining length/intersection totals;
- slack between remaining exact target and a sound/cheap residual capacity bound.

For exact targets, `less used` is not inherently better. Any hard-prune descendant would require a sound one-sided proof; an unsound but useful summary may remain ranking/retention-only.

### B. Residual topology scarcity

A state may look attractive locally while having consumed the only topology that makes later obligations compatible.

Bounded candidates:

- reachable-component capacity relative to remaining path requirement;
- bridge/cut/corridor scarcity for outstanding obligations;
- remaining independent access routes to unsatisfied landmarks;
- simple articulation-like bottleneck summaries if already cheap enough for runtime use.

Do not build a generic graph-analysis subsystem first. Prefer existing connectivity primitives or one narrowly nominated extension.

### C. Joint obligation compatibility

Recent joint-obligation work demonstrated that individually reasonable future requirements can become incompatible in combination. A similar interaction may distinguish dead retained states from live alternatives even when each individual mechanic appears feasible.

Candidate summaries should be mechanism-specific and compact, for example whether multiple outstanding must-cross / portal / intersection obligations compete for the same residual neighbor, corridor, parity class, or capacity resource.

Do not reopen the already-tested portal-terminal relocation instrument or treat `PRUNE_MC_PORTAL_FORCED_NEIGHBOR` itself as the missing answer. The question is whether a *different recurring future-compatibility distinction* exists at these exact-labelled beam decisions.

## Comparison design

Do not collapse this into `live` versus `dead` only. Preserve the search-policy context so a useful descriptor is not merely rediscovering rank.

Where the existing cases permit it, compare four conceptual cells:

1. **LIVE retained**;
2. **LIVE culled**;
3. **DEAD retained**;
4. **DEAD culled / lower-ranked**.

The strongest candidate is one that separates exact future feasibility inside the same search-status stratum, especially `DEAD retained` versus `LIVE retained` or `DEAD retained` versus `LIVE culled`, rather than simply saying retained states differ from culled states.

`R03229` contributes the especially useful `LIVE culled` + `DEAD retained` contrast. B2 supplies both recurrence and live/live controls.

## Execution discipline

1. **Existing evidence first.** Reuse the committed B1/B2 case files/results and `R03229` frozen cases. Regenerate cheap state summaries read-only if needed; do not rerun broad solver campaigns merely to decorate the table.
2. **Prespecify few descriptors.** Start with at most 2-4 concrete summaries selected from the three families above. Prefer quantities already available from current state/prune helpers.
3. **Incremental-information test.** A candidate must add information beyond current score, score margin/rank, existing hard-prune verdict, and obvious mechanic-count labels.
4. **Unrelated-parent recurrence.** One dramatic state is premise-generation only. Advancement needs the same directional distinction on unrelated exact-labelled parents.
5. **Role before implementation.** Classify a surviving descriptor as one of: sound one-sided prune candidate; unsound ranking/retention signal; expensive offline diagnostic; or evidence of an architectural representation gap.
6. **Smallest live test only after survival.** A descriptor that fails offline exact-labelled separation closes cheaply. A survivor earns the smallest matched-work shadow/live treatment that can test solve value.

## Advancement and stop conditions

### Advance when

- one compact legal descriptor repeatedly separates exact-dead preferred states from exact-live alternatives on unrelated parents;
- the effect is not explained by current score/rank, existing prune outcome, or a single mechanic family;
- the descriptor has a plausible runtime cost and intervention role;
- the claim is specific enough to define a bounded shadow/A-B test.

### Close this route when

- candidate summaries mostly duplicate existing prune/scoring information;
- live/dead separation is inconsistent across the exact-labelled controls;
- useful separation requires near-exact residual solving or a large learned model;
- the only apparent predictor is historical level identity, hint path, family membership, or other illegal runtime information.

A clean null is useful. It would say the current exact recurrence exposes a deeper architectural/horizon problem rather than a missing compact state summary, and WS2 should seek a different premise instead of feature accretion.
