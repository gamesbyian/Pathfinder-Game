Of those 649 no-T1-success rows, **635** belong to the **888 baseline production-unsolved** levels. The remaining **14** were baseline production-solved, demonstrating why T1 zero-success must not be silently equated with logical hardness, production inability, or new cold solve opportunity. The retained README independently states 253/888 production-unsolved were isolated-T1-solved at its historical budget, leaving 635 without a T1 success. The 50M-node historic budget differs from the newer **10M canonical-work** PG-I technique-response experiment. The original run's summary reports 78,505 unique cells and 48 deduplicated result rows; it explicitly notes a still-partial shard. Source coverage should therefore be verified at the *attempted technique × independent parent* grain before treating any no-success as all-technique failure.

The separate [PG-I six-family census](2026-10-17-pg-i-parent-grouped-response-census-preflight-001.md) observed 39/125 technique-vector swaps, including eight unchanged-count swaps in two independent parents, but its selected cohort has **zero variants** that defeat all five comparable isolated methods. The historical 635-parent reservoir is a material change of population and should be explored with the **same** current-code five-method equal-work measurement; not with 635 × every algorithm run by default.

## Economical selection sequence

1. Reconcile the two retained technique-census epochs (`32240161854` and `33717910218`) by identity and attempted policy, not by assuming old no-success statuses are current. The other README reports 611 baseline production-unsolved with no isolated successes in its epoch, already showing that historic producer details matter.
2. Inspect the underlying *per-method attempted/fail/censored* rows for complete comparable selected outcomes. A per-level success list alone cannot tell whether missing T1 methods were unattempted, timed out, failed a strict work cap or were merely absent from one collection.
3. Freeze a small **different-parent** cohort by deterministic salted ID hash before any current outcomes, while excluding the six PG-I response-enriched parents and PG-G selected symmetry microscope families. Keep parent-level independent units; if variants are unavailable for nominated parents, first measure the five isolated method responses on unmodified parents without manufacturing a broad family campaign.
4. If current-code five-method non-success actually occurs, only then test tiny controlled structural transformations and ask whether their technique identity changes. Preserve success, explicit measured failure, and abstention separately; compare under one true canonical-work budget and do not infer cold production gains until a current-state consumer is demonstrated.

**No current-code cold solves, algorithm changes, CP-SAT calls or new GHA were performed.** This rejoin exposes a concrete retained candidate reservoir and prevents the false conclusion that PG-I must generate another enormous variant batch to find robust-hard parents.
