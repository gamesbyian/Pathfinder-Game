The raw source experiment was a **passive probe** after the existing ordinary connectivity check in the shared hard-prune path. It computed the already-audited BC1-VX stranded-fresh-volume theorem without spending solver canonical work, and counted the stricter case in which removing goal-free stranded cells would violate the remaining volume requirement. This is a positive **exposure** result for the shared DFS/admissible seam, not yet a proof of incremental use after *all* existing prunes, nor proof that a second connectivity/Tarjan pass repays CPU overhead. Observation produced only 16 isolated method/parent attempts at a nominal **25,000 canonical-work ceiling each**, with bounded atomic overshoots; do not confuse the 25k screening dose with the original 250k-work preregistration.

## Proposed successor, without unearned promotion

The next experiment is not another eight-level full repeated scan or an immediate DFS/admissible opt-in switch. First, show the intended rejection survives a proper **existing-hard-prune overlap audit** and typed resource semantics: count how many of the 535 events are already rejected by a subsequent lower-bound, parity, mandatory obligation, portal/goal condition or other current hard-prune path, and how much descendant work such a rejection avoids. Measure selective BC1-VX setup/scan overhead in a strict-work-bounded, read-only setting. Only if both have worthwhile headroom should the line buy independent level-blind parent confirmation and source-isolated, sound, work-matched DFS/admissible treatment A/B. Keep repair opted out unless its stochastic survivor dynamics are separately tested.

The BC1-VX **beam** rule remains default-ON with explicit rollback. A distinct shared-stage optimization has **not** been promoted. The eight PG-H development parents were deliberately reused as a mechanism exposure screen, so they are not an unseen-population estimate.
