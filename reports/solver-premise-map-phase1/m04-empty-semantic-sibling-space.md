# M4 — empty semantic sibling space

Snapshot: `solver-premise-map-v1-2026-09-17` (`e9601ffb8fa304d5ea91d054a9de6cb1bf8ff28e`)
Method: preregistered M4 only. Inputs were the four frozen semantic-sibling families in the hardening overlay, their tested forms, and semantically adjacent frozen propositions. A sibling counts only when it changes the operation or representation materially, not when it merely renames a tested form.

## S001 — joint-feasibility parent P032

Tested forms: P033 scalar residual descriptors and P034 categorical event vocabularies. Both are closed tested forms that narrow P032 without closing it.

The overlay names five open sibling directions. Cross-checking the frozen map shows that several are not actually conceptually empty:

- per-instance minimal conflict/core has adjacent representation in P081/P035, although those premises concern causal DEAD-state explanations rather than a general joint-feasibility oracle;
- shared-resource assignment has substantial adjacent representation in P157/P163;
- separator/interface feasibility has adjacent representation in P014/P074/P093;
- order-class feasibility has adjacent representation in P091/P149/P169;
- axis/crossing assignment is less directly represented and appears closest to a genuinely unnamed operation.

The important M4 result is therefore not “five missing ideas.” The family contains a mixture of relation/schema holes, scope splits, and at least one comparatively empty semantic slot. P032's tested-form inventory understates how much sibling territory is already represented elsewhere in the map under different parents.

Alternative interpretation: P081/P035 and P157/P163 may be too operationally different to count as siblings of P032. If siblinghood requires a common callable interface rather than shared semantic pressure, more of S001 remains empty.

Confidence: high on overlap; medium on exact sibling boundaries. Classification: mostly `RELATION_ONLY` / `SCOPE_SPLIT`; axis/crossing assignment is a `CANDIDATE_NEW_PREMISE` output only.

## S002 — equivalence parent P011

Tested/related forms: P052 operational duplicate detection, P179 policy/work-relative equivalence, P180 dominance with work/continuation/knowledge.

The overlay's proof-bearing dominance is substantially represented by P156/P180. Policy-relative partial order and continuation-right-aware equivalence are substantially represented by P179/P180. Those are therefore not empty sibling spaces, though their relation to P011 could be made more explicit.

“Experience similarity without merge” is materially different. It asks whether two states can share evidence or policy priors while remaining semantically distinct for retention. The frozen map has nearby P167 operational similarity and P194 work-history-sensitive action choice, but no clean proposition that separates *knowledge transfer similarity* from *merge equivalence*.

Alternative interpretation: P167 already covers this if “operationally similar” is read broadly enough. That reading is possible, but P167's evidence is policy-conditioned and trace-censored, while the sibling operation would explicitly preserve separate state identity.

Confidence: medium-high. Classification: `CANDIDATE_NEW_PREMISE` for experience-similarity-without-merge, with strong `SCOPE_SPLIT` alternative.

## S003 — first-loss parent P135

Tested/related form: P183 ties first irreversible loss to a minimal counterfactual intervention and is constrained by P161 solution multiplicity.

The apparent empty siblings are again partly occupied elsewhere:

- alternate-witness first loss is explicitly anticipated by P150/P161 and P183's witness-relative caveat;
- first lost completion regime is adjacent to P013/P053/P184;
- first irreversible resource overcommitment is adjacent to P157/P163/P180;
- minimal intervention set is partly embodied by P183 itself.

What remains thin is not the existence of these concepts, but a typed specialization of P135 that turns each into a concrete diagnostic output. The frozen map contains the nouns but not consistently the `TESTED_FORM_OF`, `SPECIALIZATION_OF`, or producer/consumer relations that would make the sibling family operationally legible.

Alternative interpretation: forcing all these diagnostics under P135 could over-unify distinct causal questions. Completion-regime loss and resource overcommitment may deserve separate parents rather than sibling status.

Confidence: high. Classification: `RELATION_ONLY` with possible `ONTOLOGY_ISSUE` around diagnostic-output typing.

## S004 — solve-local knowledge parent P162

The overlay names positive forced-fact store, negative conflict store, frontier continuation state, uncertainty/calibration state, and cross-technique evidence transfer.

Frozen-map coverage is uneven:

- frontier continuation state is represented by P122/P178/P177/P200;
- uncertainty/calibration state is represented by P044/P148/P185/P194;
- cross-technique transfer is represented by P083/P177/P193/P200;
- provenance constraints on reusable facts are represented by P189;
- negative causal/conflict knowledge is represented by P081/P147;
- positive derived facts exist conceptually in P155/P162/P164 but are less concretely represented as a persistent store with consumers.

The semantic gap is therefore strongest in the *memory contract*: positive facts and negative conflicts have different validity/provenance semantics, yet the map mostly represents “sharing” and “causal explanation” as operations rather than explicit stores with lifetimes and admissible consumers.

Alternative interpretation: explicit stores are implementation forms and should not become premise-level objects until evidence shows lifetime/consumer semantics matter independently of sharing behavior.

Confidence: medium-high. Classification: `SCOPE_SPLIT` / `CANDIDATE_NEW_PREMISE` for store-semantics only, not for the already represented transfer concepts.

## M4 bounded conclusion

The preregistered sibling inventory is not four empty neighborhoods. Much of the apparently missing space is already present elsewhere in the frozen map but lacks explicit sibling relations or shares only part of the parent operation. The clearest semantically distinct underrepresented forms are: axis/crossing-assignment feasibility under joint feasibility, experience similarity without merge, and explicit lifetime/consumer semantics for positive versus negative solve-local knowledge.

These remain mining outputs only. They are not assigned IDs, added to the map, or used as input to M5–M12.
