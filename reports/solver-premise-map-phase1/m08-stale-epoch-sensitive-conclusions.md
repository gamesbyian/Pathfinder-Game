# M8 — stale or epoch-sensitive conclusions

Snapshot: `solver-premise-map-v1-2026-09-17` (`e9601ffb8fa304d5ea91d054a9de6cb1bf8ff28e`)
Method: preregistered M8 only. Historical negatives and economic conclusions were inspected against the frozen temporal-validity dimensions: code epoch, architecture epoch, production boundary, work contract, corpus/residual population, and instrumentation regime. Classification is `portable`, `narrowed but usable`, `freshness evidence needed`, or `no longer decision-bearing`. Age alone is never evidence of staleness.

## Portable at the semantic level, with empirical magnitude still epoch-bound

### P031 — production pruning must preserve a sound invariant

The claim that a sound pruning rule cannot remove semantically valid continuations is portable as a correctness requirement. What particular invariant is implemented, how expensive it is, and how often it matters remain code/architecture dependent.

Classification: `portable` principle, implementation evidence separately epoch-sensitive.

### P137 — failure of one representation does not close the parent premise

This is a research-inference guard rather than a solver-performance result. It remains portable across architecture epochs unless the parent and tested form are proven semantically equivalent.

Classification: `portable`.

### P153 — exhaustion and censoring are distinct

The distinction remains valid across epochs. The concrete condition that establishes exhaustion is work-contract and architecture dependent, but a budget-limited null cannot silently become a completeness result.

Classification: `portable` inference rule with epoch-specific proof obligation.

## Narrowed but usable

### P040 — ordering can improve finite-work solve yield

The broad finite-budget proposition is strongly supported and semantically portable. Specific heuristic rankings, effect sizes, and residual gains are architecture/residual-population dependent. Historical ordering successes remain evidence for the existence of ranking leverage, not current matched-cost magnitude.

Classification: `narrowed but usable` for historical quantitative conclusions; portable parent.

### P051 — coarse portal merge is positive but placement-sensitive

The frozen evidence records a positive treatment in a specific earned residual and placement context, including a dead-last additive result. The mechanism remains decision-bearing as evidence that a portal-oriented treatment can add solves, but the magnitude and best placement require the same work/portfolio context.

Classification: `narrowed but usable`; freshness variables are residual population, predecessor set, work contract, and stage placement.

### P100 — repair differs from DFS/beam

The evidence supports repair as behaviorally distinct under tested conditions. Current economics, complementarity, and best invocation point remain architecture/residual dependent.

Classification: `narrowed but usable`.

### P166 — tested randomization treatment

The tested randomization form can remain closed for its exact implementation and dose while broader seed/restart sensitivity (P165) remains diagnostic. Architecture changes that alter candidate generation or tie-breaking could reopen empirical portability of the implementation, not erase the historical result.

Classification: `narrowed but usable` at tested-form scope.

## Freshness evidence needed before present-tense decision authority

### P005 and historical negative families generally

P005 explicitly rejects unconditional portability. A historical negative must name the architecture epoch, production boundary, work contract, corpus/residual, and instrumentation relevant to its conclusion. P181/P182 add a second problem: the residual itself is endogenous to prior capabilities and portfolio order.

Classification: `freshness evidence needed` unless the conclusion is a purely semantic impossibility result.

### P021 and older lexical/generator conclusions

Historical lexical expansion evidence is tied to the generator vocabulary, downstream ranking/retention behavior, and survivor population. P190/P191 also show that generator adequacy depends on the dimensions represented and construction-witness distribution.

Classification: `freshness evidence needed` for current solve-value claims; historical mechanism evidence remains usable.

### P041/P042 and reduced ranking/rescoring forms

Whole-puzzle ranking and rescoring conclusions are sensitive to candidate distribution, downstream retention, and the current residual. P043 further questions whether the scalar objective itself matches retained-set value.

Classification: `freshness evidence needed` for current economics, not automatically reopened as feature ideas.

### P167 — operational similarity from traces

Trace-based similarity depends on current policy and is vulnerable to censoring and instrumentation changes. A similarity conclusion from an older search policy can cease to represent current continuation behavior even if the underlying puzzle states are unchanged.

Classification: `freshness evidence needed`; dimensions include architecture, work contract, residual and instrumentation.

### P171 — removed tooling around unresolved premises

Where an unresolved premise can only be tested by resurrecting removed tooling, an old negative cannot automatically stand in for current evidence. Reconstitution must preserve enough of the original treatment to make the comparison meaningful while still testing the current architecture.

Classification: `freshness evidence needed` before promotion or renewed closure.

## No conclusion proven no longer decision-bearing

M8 found no frozen historical result that can be responsibly classified as wholly `no longer decision-bearing` from temporal drift alone. The hardening authority correctly treats staleness as conditional. Some results may eventually land in that category after a concrete epoch comparison, but Phase 1 has no license to infer it from date, removed code, or a changed residual by itself.

## M8 bounded conclusion

Temporal validity is multi-dimensional. The strongest recurring split is between portable semantic/inference rules and epoch-bound empirical magnitude/economics. The frozen map already encodes much of this caution in P005, P181/P182, P187/P188 and the hardening overlay; the remaining risk is applying historical conclusions without carrying those qualifiers into present decisions.
