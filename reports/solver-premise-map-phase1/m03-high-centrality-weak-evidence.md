# M3 — high-centrality weak evidence

Snapshot: `solver-premise-map-v1-2026-09-17` (`e9601ffb8fa304d5ea91d054a9de6cb1bf8ff28e`)
Method: preregistered M3 only. The frozen auditor's high-centrality/open-status set was crossed with each proposition's frozen status, evidence summary, and evidence-dependency relations. This is epistemic leverage analysis, not feature prioritization.

## Findings

### M3.1 — P032: high structural load, parent remains open after two closed forms

P032 has the highest reported degree (10) and `very high` weight, but remains `high-value open`. P033 and P034 are closed tested forms and both explicitly `NARROWS` P032 rather than falsifying it. P163 supports P032 through shared-resource competition and P169 complements it through commutativity.

Weakness type: the evidence is semantically heterogeneous. Two compact universal descriptors failed, while broader per-instance relational mechanisms remain open. The proposition is therefore central but not uniformly weak; rather, its evidence strongly rejects some forms and weakly resolves the parent.

Alternative interpretation: repeated compact-form failures may indicate the parent is too broad to be decision-bearing without a sharper operation. That would be an ontology/formulation problem rather than simply “need more evidence.”

Confidence: high. Classification: `SCOPE_SPLIT` / possible `ONTOLOGY_ISSUE` for later review.

### M3.2 — P024: high diagnostic centrality with an under-observed empirical partition

P024 has degree 6, `very high` weight, and `major under-observed question` status. It partitions current misses into candidate absent, present-but-unreachable, and reached-then-lost. Multiple research families depend on this distinction because their mechanisms act at different loci.

Weakness type: observability. The map has richer evidence for later-stage extinction than for source absence, so the diagnostic partition is structurally load-bearing while incompletely measured.

Alternative interpretation: exact accepted-target lineage may be too expensive or witness-sensitive to serve as a universal diagnostic. P135/P161/P150 already show that a simple lineage can misattribute first loss when alternatives exist.

Confidence: high. Classification: `DESCRIPTIVE` epistemic leverage.

### M3.3 — P063: central adaptive-control parent with no general replanning loop

P063 has degree 6, `very high` weight, and `underexplored` status. Its evidence is architectural absence: staged diversification exists, but no general online failure-mode inference/replanning loop exists. Incoming relations touch routing granularity, predecessor context, trust, information-valued actions, and abandonment.

Weakness type: representability/participation. There is no single deployed object whose causal value can be evaluated as “P063.” This makes the parent central but operationally diffuse.

Alternative interpretation: existing staged diversification may already realize much of the useful parent behavior without a unified controller. A monolithic control-loop formulation could be ontology convenience rather than missing capability.

Confidence: medium-high. Classification: `ONTOLOGY_ISSUE` candidate / `DESCRIPTIVE`.

### M3.4 — P081/P082: central failure-learning chain with limited causal evidence

P081 (degree 6) is `open / early evidence`; P082 (degree 5) is `major open`. P035 is one bounded tested form under P081 and is blocked by population/contrast quality. P147 is a prerequisite for P082, meaning causal attribution must precede selective commitment revision.

Weakness type: causal validity and population support. There is some evidence that relaxing commitments can revive DEAD states, but little evidence that compact causes generalize enough within a solve to support reusable backjump/revision operations.

Alternative interpretation: causal explanation may pay primarily as a diagnostic or local repair selector rather than a reusable constraint system. The parent should not inherit production authority from small offline probes.

Confidence: high. Classification: `DESCRIPTIVE`; maturity/authority conditioning required.

### M3.5 — P177: interface centrality rests mostly on architectural observation

P177 (degree 6) is a `major underasked parent premise` with `very high` weight. Its core evidence is that architecture has distinct stage/attempt boundaries and that the map historically modeled loci more strongly than interfaces. P178 supplies a concrete frontier-loss case; P193 proposes explicit handoff contracts.

Weakness type: causal attribution. It is plausible that useful information dies at interfaces, but the broad proposition is not yet quantified by lost solves or by a census of which artifacts matter at which handoff.

Alternative interpretation: module-local lifetimes may often be correct economics. The existence of a boundary does not prove harmful information loss.

Confidence: high for under-observation, medium for solve impact. Classification: `DESCRIPTIVE`.

### M3.6 — P013: completion-regime representation has broad descendants but little direct evidence

P013 (degree 4) is `largely untested`, `high` weight. It enables P053 and relates to P092/P140. Its evidence is primarily absence of a first-class completion-regime object and conceptual pressure from optionality/retention questions.

Weakness type: construct validity. The map has not established that a compact, stable “completion regime” representation exists, much less that it is predictive and economical.

Alternative interpretation: regime language may be a useful analyst description of diversity while the solver needs only local constraints or dominance relations. A new object may add ontology complexity without capability.

Confidence: high that evidence is thin; low-to-medium on necessity of explicit representation. Classification: `DESCRIPTIVE` / possible `ONTOLOGY_ISSUE`.

### M3.7 — P065: allocation objective is central but estimator legality/economics are unresolved

P065 (degree 4) says marginal new solves per work is preferable to raw stage success; status is `implicit / partly practiced`. P123 requires comparable work accounting; P175 says net solve count can hide capability churn.

Weakness type: measurement and estimability. The objective is conceptually plausible, but online estimation without historical identity leakage and with predecessor-conditioned residuals remains unresolved.

Alternative interpretation: the objective may be correct only at portfolio evaluation time, not as an online scheduling statistic. The same formula can have different authority depending on when and from what data it is estimated.

Confidence: high. Classification: `SCOPE_SPLIT` by decision role/authority.

## M3 bounded conclusion

The frozen graph's structurally central weak/open premises fail for different reasons: parent breadth (P032), observability (P024), missing operational object (P063/P013), causal/population support (P081/P082), interface attribution (P177), and estimator authority (P065). Treating them as one “weak evidence” class would erase the discriminating question each requires.

No central premise is promoted to the solver queue or treated as a priority by this lens.
