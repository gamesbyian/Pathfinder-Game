# M12 — ontology escapees

Snapshot: `solver-premise-map-v1-2026-09-17` (`e9601ffb8fa304d5ea91d054a9de6cb1bf8ff28e`)
Method: preregistered M12 only. Each of the six frozen ontology stress-test decompositions was applied as an alien lens to the map's ordinary locus/claim/scope/evidence/relation vocabulary plus the hardening authority/maturity overlays. The question is whether an important research question cannot be represented cleanly, not whether a new column would be aesthetically convenient.

## O001 — information flow

Question: for each expensive fact, who creates it, who may consume it, with what authority, for how long, and where is it lost?

The frozen map contains strong semantic anchors: P162 knowledge sharing, P177 handoff loss, P189 provenance, P193 explicit contracts, P197 authority, P200 lifetime/transfer radius. What it lacks is a compact way to bind these properties to one artifact flow. Ordinary relations can say `ENABLES`, `PREREQUISITE`, or `EVIDENCE_DEPENDS_ON`, but they do not directly encode producer, consumer, lifetime, transfer boundary, invalidation rule, and permission together.

Escape assessment: genuine cross-cutting representation dimension, though it may be better implemented as typed interface metadata than as a new premise taxonomy axis.

Alternative interpretation: sufficiently explicit stage-contract documents could carry this information without extending the premise ontology itself.

Classification: `ONTOLOGY_ISSUE`. Confidence: high.

## O002 — decision rights

Question: which actor may prune, rank, route, stop, revise, validate, or only observe on the basis of each information class?

P197 directly repairs much of this escape in the hardening layer by naming authority classes. P031, P151, P187, P189 and P198 further constrain promotion and use. However, authority is mostly represented as a global vocabulary rather than attached to every fact-producing premise or relation.

Escape assessment: no longer an unnamed conceptual dimension, but still external to most canonical premise rows and graph edges. The remaining issue is application, not discovery.

Alternative interpretation: authority belongs to code/API contracts and experiment protocols, not premise-map records. If so, the ontology only needs references to the governing authority scheme.

Classification: `ONTOLOGY_ISSUE` partly repaired by the frozen hardening overlay. Confidence: high.

## O003 — irreversibility

Question: which commitments are reversible under remaining work and which irreversibly destroy completion regimes?

P135 and P183 represent first irreversible loss directly. P082/P147 represent selective revision, P184 represents completion-regime option value, and P180 incorporates continuation rights into dominance. Irreversibility can therefore be expressed within the existing map as a semantic property of a commitment plus a counterfactual intervention.

Escape assessment: weak escape. The concept is important but does not currently require a new ontology dimension; typed relations or tags around reversible/irreversible commitments may improve discoverability.

Alternative interpretation: if irreversibility becomes a pervasive property spanning generation, pruning, ranking, retention and budgeting, a dedicated relation/property could reduce repeated prose.

Classification: `RELATION_ONLY` / vocabulary improvement, not a demonstrated missing ontology dimension. Confidence: high.

## O004 — causal intervention

Question: what minimal intervention changes the reachable accepted-solution set at each failure locus?

P147/P183 explicitly introduce causal earlier commitments and minimal counterfactual intervention. P175's solved-set churn and P192's compositional credit also resist purely observational attribution. Yet the evidence schema does not systematically distinguish observational association, controlled treatment, counterfactual replay, exact intervention, or causal proof.

Escape assessment: the *question* is representable, but the *evidence method/authority* for causal claims is under-typed. Two premises with similar evidence summaries can carry very different causal force.

Alternative interpretation: P197 authority classes and ordinary source provenance may be sufficient if reports state study design rigorously. A dedicated causal-method field could duplicate experiment documentation.

Classification: `ONTOLOGY_ISSUE` in evidence-method vocabulary, not necessarily proposition taxonomy. Confidence: medium-high.

## O005 — resource conservation

Question: which scarce future resources are jointly consumed by otherwise independent obligations?

P157/P163 encode shared future resources/capacity contention; P032 is the broader joint-feasibility parent; P180 can include continuation rights/work resources. The existing domain/mechanic-resource fields can name concrete scarce resources.

Escape assessment: not a genuine ontology escape at v1. The map can represent the substantive question, although resource *accounting structure* may need specialized representations for particular mechanics.

Alternative interpretation: a general conservation-law relation could expose similarities across portal slots, crossing rights, parity budgets, separator capacity, and work budget. That is a possible abstraction, not a demonstrated schema requirement.

Classification: `DESCRIPTIVE`; no new dimension established. Confidence: high.

## O006 — role separation

Question: which claims belong to solver, observer, exact oracle, referee, generator, scheduler, or research analyst, and what breaks if roles are conflated?

The frozen map has multiple role-specific concepts: validation/referee truth, exact query P159, observer diagnostics P135/P188, scheduler/controller P061/P063/P197, generator P020/P190, and analyst-only evidence. P197's authority classes are strongly relevant, but `system_locus` does not consistently distinguish actor role from runtime stage or data locus.

Escape assessment: genuine vocabulary ambiguity rather than a wholly missing semantic dimension. “Where in the system?” and “which actor is allowed to make this claim/decision?” are not the same coordinate.

Alternative interpretation: actor role can be modeled entirely through authority plus source/consumer relations, avoiding a new first-class field.

Classification: `SCOPE_SPLIT` / `ONTOLOGY_ISSUE` in role vocabulary. Confidence: high.

## Cross-stress-test result

The six alien decompositions do not reveal six missing ontology axes. They separate into four outcomes:

1. **Genuine cross-cutting representation pressure:** information flow (O001).
2. **Conceptual dimensions already made explicit by hardening but not attached uniformly:** decision rights (O002).
3. **Evidence/role vocabulary gaps:** causal intervention method (O004) and role separation (O006).
4. **Important questions already representable in the current ontology:** irreversibility (O003) and resource conservation (O005).

A further pattern is that the strongest escapees describe *relationships among facts, actors, lifetimes, permissions and evidence*, rather than new solver loci. That is compatible with the hardening report's warning that the original map was stronger at nodes than at interfaces and governance.

Alternative interpretation: this pattern may be a consequence of the hardening overlay itself having been designed to probe relational weaknesses. M12 therefore does not infer that the canonical ontology should be rebuilt around interfaces.

## M12 boundary note

No schema, premise, relation, or frozen input was changed. M12 identifies representation pressure only. It does not propose the v2 ontology, admit new premise IDs, or use its findings to rerun M1-M11.
