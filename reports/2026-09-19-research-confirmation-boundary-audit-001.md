## Boundary under audit

The work-ladder line had meaningful selection pressure:

- it was chosen from several live post-D1 discriminator families;
- the historical zero-compute reuse was explicitly treated as confounded discovery evidence rather than confirmation;
- a new current-commit level-blind slice was required before making the scoped underdose decision.

Before dispatch, the preflight froze:

- the 20-id population;
- deterministic selection rule;
- 300M and 1.2B tiers;
- corpus and level-blind instrument;
- three-branch decision rule;
- treatment interpretation.

No outcome from the confirmation slice was inspected before that precommitment.

## Why procedural untouchedness is sufficient here

The confirmation produced a positive narrow fact:

- 0/20 solved at 300M;
- 3/20 solved at 1.2B.

That supported only:

> recoverable capacity exists at the higher dose on this slice.

It explicitly did **not** license a production budget promotion.

The follow-on economics question then reused already-published work telemetry and an already-published EW1 comparator table.

Its rejection margin is enormous:

- actual 4x cost per marginal recovered solve: ~14.1B canonical work units;
- known comparator techniques: 24x to 635x cheaper;
- even the factually impossible assumption that all 20 levels solved at 1.2B leaves escalation at least 3.6x more expensive than the priciest listed comparator.

This robustness matters to the confirmation boundary.

A designer having seen exact confirmation outcomes before the economics calculation cannot plausibly turn the narrow negative economics sign positive by choosing a favorable recovery interpretation; the report explicitly evaluates the maximally favorable impossible bound.

The decision is also **rejection / no promotion**, not a broad capability claim whose optimistic selection would be amplified into production.

For this exact decision, procedural precommitment plus the robustness bound is sufficient.

## What this does not establish

This audit does not claim machine-verifiable untouchedness.

It does not say the optional hard-blind mode is unnecessary in general.

Hard-blind confirmation remains appropriate when:

- many candidates were tuned before a broad promotion;
- exact holdout outcomes could materially change the selected treatment;
- claim scope is broad enough that procedural trust is insufficient;
- the aggregate prespecified verdict can be exposed before forensic IDs/traces without compromising the decision.

None of those conditions currently changes the work-ladder/economics disposition.

## Disposition

Part VIII's confirmation-boundary requirement is satisfied for one real selected decision lineage:

- procedural untouchedness is explicitly adequate for the scoped decision;
- limitations are recorded;
- no stronger untouchedness claim is made;
- hard-blind infrastructure is not built without a consumer.
