# Solver archaeology: rename-aware reverse pass

> **Status:** concluded-positive
> **Last evidence:** 2026-09-13 — the rename-aware pass established the relevant vocabulary lineages, historical validity checks, and orphan/premise distinctions now preserved in the archaeology register.
> **Decision:** use rename-aware reverse tracing as a permanent archaeology method; current solver priority remains owned by `docs/solver-optimization-workstreams.md`.
> **Remaining gate:** none

## Question

What historical solver evidence becomes visible or changes interpretation once the August/September naming migration is treated as a semantic translation layer rather than a textual rename?

## Method

Historical solver claims were re-read through the current vocabulary contract and post-naming compatibility rules. Searches covered commit history and surviving planning/evidence around:

- homotopy/topological diversity;
- archetype/routing-regime studies;
- diverse-beam/mechanic-bucket retention;
- dedup/coarse-state merge and near-tie retention;
- compact/canonical attempt identities;
- oracle/atlas/labelled-branch/reference-model terminology.

The pass distinguishes premise failure from implementation failure and checks whether old labels collapsed dimensions now represented separately as search family, scoring profile, structural bias, beam retention, routing regime, stage, resource envelope, and seed.

## Findings

### 1. Homotopy remains an apparent orphan, not a renamed descendant

The July sequence is unusually clean:

1. external-literature review nominated homotopy-class path signatures as a genuine gap;
2. the first cell-overlap proxy was explicitly rejected as a mis-designed test;
3. a corrected winding-number probe found multiple homotopy classes on 12/19 must-cross-heavy published levels;
4. 16.6% of cross-class hint pairs were classified as similar by the then-current curation diversity metric;
5. the item became the strongest-evidence candidate and was called the highest-priority research build in the July 23 planning consolidation.

No later clean implementation/closure has yet been found, and the naming compatibility maps do not identify homotopy as a renamed current mechanism.

**Interpretation:** the historical evidence proves a missing solution-diversity axis, not solve-rate value. The smallest modern rejoin is topology-at-extinction on exact-labelled LIVE/DEAD cases.

### 2. Historical `dedup` evidence is actually representation-loss evidence

Naming cleanup changed `STRATEGY_DEDUP_NEAR_TIE_*` to `STRATEGY_COARSE_STATE_NEAR_TIE_*` because the mechanism intentionally merges non-identical states. This is not exact duplicate removal.

The history therefore reads differently:

- near-tie retention changed which compressed-state survivor was kept and produced gains and losses;
- a dead-last additive retry under the alternate retention representation eventually validated as a clean positive population mechanism after subtractive reserve designs caused collateral starvation;
- portal coarse-state merge later produced a large positive basin but failed global promotion on a real retained-capability regression;
- R01273 forensics localized the first loss to information omitted from the coarse key. Bounded second-survivor and predecessor-lookback salvage delayed extinction but did not converge and was correctly closed.

**Interpretation:** alternate compressed representations own different capabilities. This supports a general search question about preserving qualitatively different completion regimes, without reopening the closed global merge forms.

### 3. Old `diverse beam` language maps to mechanic-bucket retention, not generic diversity

Historical compact identities using `(diverse)` correspond to current mechanic-bucket retention. A later fixed-pair Corpus-1 pilot found portal-use-signature diversity moved in the same direction for mechanic-buckets-only versus plain-only wins across both prespecified pairs, but disagreement samples were too small and the experiment stopped as inconclusive.

**Interpretation:** preserve as weak evidence that categorical state/history distinctions can matter. It does not reopen generic beam-retention work, which remains closed in tested forms.

### 4. `Archetype` studies split cleanly into composition versus acquisition once translated to routing regime

The retired `archetype` term described a hand-authored routing classifier, not an intrinsic puzzle type. This matters for old conclusions.

One July turn-landmark-heavy study bypassed the shared ladder and gave all 16 existing attempt configurations independent full budgets. Every configuration still timed out after substantial genuine search. The historical conclusion was explicitly that a cheap routing reorder could not solve the population.

**Interpretation:** this is evidence of a capability-acquisition gap, not merely failed routing. Old archetype reports should be classified by whether they tested action selection or exhausted the known action set.

### 5. Vocabulary drift has already created a real zero-participation analysis

After naming cleanup, `analyze-technique-relative-advantage.mjs` still contained compact historical attempt keys such as `beam:objectiveFirst@beam5000(diverse)` while current artifacts used canonical structured identities. The analyzer silently matched zero rows for all eight prespecified pairs until the keys were translated and the report regenerated.

**Interpretation:** a successful historical/current analysis run is not evidence the intended treatments participated. Archaeology must prove normalized identity intersection and nonzero rows before trusting a null.

### 6. Oracle -> atlas -> labelled branch -> reference-label is a real historical vocabulary chain

Current compatibility guidance preserves several historical/current boundaries:

- old compact attempt/stage/routing identities are normalized at owning readers;
- old `oracle-abstain` known-prefix sources map to the current reference-model concept;
- `atlas-sweep` became labelled-branch collection;
- current CP-SAT outputs use `referenceLabel` / `referenceReason`;
- old atlas-directory spellings remain historical-only rather than executable aliases.

A related CP-SAT correction showed why this matters conceptually: an earlier report attributed abstention to `mustCross>=2`, but later investigation found the common unsupported mechanic was flipping filters. Adding flipping-filter support removed the supposed must-cross limitation.

**Interpretation:** old exact-labelled evidence must be searched across every vocabulary era, and observed level-feature correlation must not be mistaken for reference-model eligibility cause.

### 7. Treatment delivery is itself part of historical validity

Multiple historical cases reinforce a stricter validity check:

- routing/ablation flags existed but were provably inert in an isolated-technique census because the census bypassed the routing function where the flag was read;
- a turn-bias ablation flag was also inert even though the cell still tested a real turn-biased AttemptConfig;
- gate-level shared budgets could starve later gates inside a census intended to compare techniques fairly;
- an unsolved-only population made regression structurally impossible in a sound-bound evaluation;
- a sound pruning change still produced net solve losses under fixed budget because it changed heuristic survivor/order dynamics.

**Interpretation:** historical nulls and positives need execution-path, participation, population, and fixed-work checks, not just code presence and correctness witnesses.

## Updated archaeology priorities

1. **Topology at extinction:** determine whether known-live culled states preserve a completion topology missing from retained dead states.
2. **Portfolio/search-basin participation:** normalize historical portfolio/variety/restart identities and test whether nominally distinct actions actually explored distinct basins.
3. **Exact-label ancestry:** search oracle/atlas/labelled-branch/reference-label evidence for older LIVE/DEAD observations not represented in current Class-5 premise generation.
4. **Future crossing commitments:** separate the old Intersection Opportunity Graph premise from its bundled synthetic-obligation implementation.
5. **Constrained-event research oracle:** assess whether a small offline event-feasibility query can cheaply adjudicate candidate categorical completion structures.
6. **Scheduler Cartesian starvation:** retain as a permanent composition audit whenever isolated capability exists but production does not expose/reach it.

## Boundaries preserved

This pass does not reopen nearby scalar future-feasibility summaries, generic scorer tuning, universal width increases, broad retention frameworks, broad CDCL/LCG, or already-closed global coarse-state merge treatments. Historical interest is not itself a changed causal premise.
