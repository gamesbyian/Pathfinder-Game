# Level-blindness audit topology reconsideration 001

> **Status:** concluded-positive
> **Last evidence:** 2026-09-16 — merged controlled open-path topology fork result from PR #1821.
> **Decision:** no core finding from the level-blindness audit is retracted. The topology result strengthens the provenance-not-specificity correction and earns open-path topology/path-history representation as a live microscope premise.
> **Remaining gate:** determine whether value comes from a compact reusable descriptor, a generic per-instance topology derivation procedure, or neither; do not route production directly on raw phase.

## What changed

The controlled topology-fork pilot constructed same-board, same-endpoint, same-length prefix pairs from published human/editor parents before exact labelling, matched the experiment's non-history mechanic-progress controls, and exact-labelled both branches.

It produced four exact LIVE/DEAD-discordant pairs across two independent parent families, `P00124` and `P00137`. In every discordant pair, the prefixes differ by one full topological turn around a single board puncture while matching the frozen non-history controls.

This establishes, on the tested construction, that current path history contains completion-relevant topological information not captured by the matched ordinary mechanic-progress state. It does not establish prevalence on the residual population, a production feature, a hard prune, or an economic win.

## Why this strengthens the audit

The topology signal is derived entirely from the current board and current prefix. A runtime implementation would not need historical level identity, prior witnesses, stored winners, corpus position, provenance, or state from a previous solve.

Therefore it is a concrete example of the governing rule:

> **Level-blindness constrains provenance, not specificity.**

It also illustrates the corollary:

> **The procedure may generalize even when the derived topology fact is unique to one board.**

A production mechanism does not need the exact same puncture-side relation to recur on many unrelated boards merely to be level-blind. Cross-parent recurrence remains important if the candidate is one fixed reusable descriptor or routing rule. It is not a universal prerequisite for a generic topology algorithm whose output is board-specific.

## Successor routes

The result earns two legitimate, separately gated research routes.

### Route A: compact reusable descriptor

Search for a compact invariant relation that recurs across independent parents and predicts completion fate beyond generic depth/difficulty. Candidate forms might include a normalized puncture-side commitment or other low-dimensional topology state.

Advance only with:

- a defensible invariance definition;
- endpoint/reference controls;
- recurrence beyond the two pilot parents;
- predictive value on independent evidence;
- bounded construction/use cost;
- end-to-end solve/work value.

### Route B: generic per-instance topology reasoning

Study a generic current-input procedure whose output may differ on every board. Candidate outputs include:

- winding/phase state around board-specific punctures;
- separator-side commitment;
- region accessibility conditioned on path history;
- topology-aware canonicalization or state equivalence;
- exact impossibility facts derived from current topology;
- decomposition/interface contracts informed by current path history.

This route does not require one semantic relation to recur. The procedure itself must be sound for its proposed use and must show useful behavior across independent evaluation levels. Its construction and maintenance cost still matter.

## What is not earned

The pilot does **not** justify:

- routing production directly on the raw phase coordinate;
- assuming all Class-5 failures are topological;
- treating one puncture construction as a universal topology representation;
- weakening the frozen H1 experiment;
- building a topology-aware search architecture before the microscope identifies the smallest useful representation/action;
- skipping independent confirmation or economics because the relation is level-blind legal.

Legality, soundness, predictive value, and economics remain separate gates.

## Priority interaction

Open-path topology is premise-earned and therefore belongs at the top of the immediate microscope/acquisition line in the current workstream authority. H1 remains the strongest independent next line and its frozen prespec remains valid.

The topology result changes the interpretation of the successor, not the research discipline. H1 and DEAD-core work should retain the same distinction between:

- a recurring compact relation suitable for reusable abstraction; and
- a generic per-instance derivation procedure whose outputs may be board-specific.

## Decision

The topology result does not contradict the original overshoot audit. It materially reinforces it.

Future solver agents should therefore avoid collapsing the topology successor into “find one compact generic descriptor.” The correct live question is broader: determine the smallest sound and economical current-input topology procedure, whether its useful output is reusable across boards or derived uniquely for each one.
