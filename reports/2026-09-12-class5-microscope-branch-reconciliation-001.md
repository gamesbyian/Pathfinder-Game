## Freshness reconciliation and provenance correction

The raw freshness scan initially found 36/431 class-5 rows with later `isolatedTechnique` hint successes, apparently suggesting a large cheap composition/exposure harvest. That interpretation did not survive protocol reconciliation.

All 59 corresponding level/action pairs were already present and failed in the frozen T1 census. The mismatch came from a provenance-integrity seam: technique-census hint capture records `isolatedTechnique: true` and the winning solver action, but historically did not preserve the source census cell. A successful T1 variant, paired cell, or ablation could therefore later resemble evidence that the bare T1 action itself solved the level.

Exact path + attempt-identity matching against the originating frozen census resolved the same-solver-revision suspicious cases decisively: **5/5 were T1-variant successes, 0/5 were base-T1 successes**, and all five came from `coarse-state-near-tie-retention-off` cells. Production lifecycle telemetry already offers and reaches the corresponding `coarse-state-near-tie-retention-disabled-retry`; no starvation was found. The apparent harvest is therefore not a hidden batch of known bare-T1 winners.

This is an evidence-integrity defect worth fixing at the producer: future technique-census hint provenance must retain enough cell context to distinguish base T1 from variants/pairs/ablations without reconstructive joins.
