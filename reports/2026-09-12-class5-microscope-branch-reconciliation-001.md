## Intellectual work preserved from the superseded R03351 line

The following ideas remain valid and are already incorporated into the corrected line's research direction:

1. A single hard level may be used as a **microscope**, not as a benchmark or promotion target.
2. The first useful object is the **extinction decision**, not the final unsolved outcome.
3. The comparison set should remain tiny and prespecified: known-live culled witness, rank-1 survivor, and cutoff survivor.
4. Exact/reference work is supporting adjudication, not permission to grow a new general exact-solver program.
5. A one-level solve is insufficient. Success means a reusable runtime-legal distinction or a precise architectural limitation.
6. Generic width, scorer, or arbitrary bucket-key proliferation is not licensed by one failure.
7. Because August B1/B2 already established `dead preferred / live alternative` and `live / live` extinction phenotypes, reproducing those facts is recurrence, not a new premise. The microscope must produce a materially new phenotype or a mechanism-specific discriminator that explains the recurrence.
