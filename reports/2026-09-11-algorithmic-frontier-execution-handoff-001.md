## Stop / promotion rules

- Static effects vanish after composition control -> record the null and move on.
- Joint-obligation observer fires similarly in matched class 4 and class 5 -> do not interpret mechanic concentration as frontier-specific evidence.
- Source-controlled solution structure separates strongly -> translate the strongest repeatable difference into a legal current-state mechanism hypothesis.
- Controlled family transforms repeatedly flip many action families together -> prioritize shared-capability localization.
- Controlled transforms flip only one action/operational family -> keep the result technique-specific unless stronger evidence appears.
- Class 5 disproportionately rejects or aliases demonstrated live prefixes -> prioritize correctness/state-equivalence diagnosis according to the actual loss mechanism.
- Viable basins survive reasoning but are rank/beam-culled -> prioritize WS4 scoring/retention/search policy.
- No coherent frontier-wide distinction -> subdivide class 5 by evidence phenotype; `unknown` is an acceptable result.
- If phenotypes vary idiosyncratically by action and no cross-action mechanism recurs, the shared-gap hypothesis is weakened and technique/config/routing research retains priority.
- No broad new census, giant solve campaign, new variant harvest, reasoning-capability corpus, or heavyweight framework until a concrete recurring mechanism warrants it.
