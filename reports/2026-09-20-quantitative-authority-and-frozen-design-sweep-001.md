## Audit rule

For a current decision-bearing experiment, ask where each of these is owned:

- population identity and independent unit;
- action/treatment/config identity;
- sample size or exact inclusion rule;
- budgets, ceilings and dose ladder;
- thresholds/cut points;
- discriminator and outcome interpretation;
- stopping, escalation and reopen rules.

The design is frozen only when later acquisition/reduction code consumes those choices or refuses to decide when they are absent. Analyzer defaults may provide mechanics; they must not silently become scientific policy.

## Current gates checked

### WS2 failure-response reconnaissance

**Result: strong / frozen enough for its present role.**

The tracked analysis contract owns the question, allowed routes, censoring policy, selection sequence, independent unit/topology, compact-instrument support policy, observability requirements, live rivals, outcome interpretation and stop rule. The reducer consumes the contract rather than inventing route semantics locally.

This is a routing/discriminator screen, not a treatment-effect experiment, so the absence of a treatment budget or numeric efficacy threshold is intentional rather than missing design.

### Class-3 exact-action dose acquisition

**Result: acceptable for acquisition; no treatment threshold is currently owed.**

The frozen expectation artifact owns the current 23-parent exact-rescuer map, and the reducer classifies participation/dose/censoring without turning missing dose into a negative. The active question is measurement completeness and phenotype, not yet an efficacy threshold. A later dose-response treatment must freeze its own quantitative design rather than inheriting isolated T1 cost as policy.

### H3 independent transfer

**Result before this pass: design leak. Result after this pass: repaired.**

Two decision-bearing choices were still deferred:

1. the report allowed a proportional subsample of the expensive reachability cohort after the phenotype screen if the full cohort was “judged too expensive”;
2. the negative branch used “statistically indistinguishable from random” without specifying a test, while the directional branch gave “e.g. >=5x” only as illustrative prose.

The preflight now has no adaptive-subsample authorization. The full phenotype-positive cohort is the default expensive-stage population; a smaller cohort requires a new pre-outcome amendment freezing exact size, seed, stratification and inclusion before reachability outcomes.

Its decision rule is now mechanical:

- transfer-positive when ascending length clears at least 50% of achievable rescues at an already-fixed budget point <=20%;
- directional-but-below-transfer-bar only at the already-fixed 2% point, requiring at least 2 solves and `ascendingLength >= 5 * randomMean` over the frozen 50 seeded random orders;
- every other complete valid outcome is no-prespecified-transfer-signal;
- incomplete/incompatible acquisition blocks rather than becoming negative.

This deliberately removes undefined significance language instead of inventing a new statistical framework after the fact.

### Lane-A C0

**Result before this pass: lifecycle leak. Result after this pass: repaired.**

The C0 result already concluded the geometric-cut-only signature is mixed and directed work to C1, while the preflight still advertised itself as active with a live combine gate. The preflight is now `superseded` and explicitly points to the C0 result/current C1 handoff.

This is a transition-coherence issue discovered by the quantitative/frozen-design sweep: a perfectly frozen old design can still become false current state after its transition completes.

## Broader finding

The most useful refinement to the taxonomy is that **quantitative authority and transition coherence should be audited together**. Decision policy has a temporal lifecycle:

1. design values become authoritative before outcome;
2. reducers must consume or validate them;
3. once the experiment resolves, the design remains historical evidence but must stop advertising an active gate;
4. successor work must freeze its own policy instead of inheriting accidental analyzer defaults.

That temporal chain is a stronger target than merely searching source code for numeric literals.

## Deliberate non-changes

No universal quantitative-policy object is introduced. The live designs differ materially: routing reconnaissance, acquisition completeness, recurrence sizing and allocation transfer do not share enough policy shape to justify one generic schema.

No historical preflight migration is attempted. A historical report with old constants is not a defect unless something current still treats those constants as live authority.
