## Why this exists

The current solver has a rich raw search state (`visited`, `edgeUsage`, crossing counts, obligation masks, portal/flipper state, etc.). The plausible deeper gap is therefore not simply “missing state.” It is **missing derived information about what the consumed path implies for future feasibility**: residual topology, remaining interfaces, coupled resource demands, and opportunity cost.

This distinction matters because many techniques/configurations share the same underlying reasoning vocabulary. If materially different actions repeatedly lose known-live material at the same residual-state boundary, better routing among those actions is unlikely to solve the underlying problem.

The converse matters equally: a hard class-5 level, mechanic concentration, or one vivid trace is not evidence of a fundamental solver gap. This map is designed to falsify that story as readily as to support it.

## Claim boundary: when to call something a shared capability hypothesis

A recurring failure may be promoted from technique-specific diagnosis to a **shared capability hypothesis** only when the evidence increasingly supports all of the following:

1. **Cross-action recurrence:** materially operationally distinct actions fail at the same kind of boundary; nearby configs of one engine do not count as independent capability evidence.
2. **Known-live grounding:** referee-valid paths/prefixes, source-controlled hints, exact/reference evidence, or controlled family siblings establish that the relevant state/basin really retained a completion; loss of a stored basin alone does not prove all alternatives dead.
3. **Residual-state mechanism:** the distinction is expressed in current state/future feasibility, not merely a whole-level correlation or historical identity label.
4. **Controlled counterfactual where available:** an existing family/symmetry/minimal transform changes the relevant structural demand and correspondingly changes the failure, with parent/family as the independent unit.
5. **Generic intervention path:** the proposed consequence can be computed level-blind from current level/state/search information and is not a saved-solution/census lookup.
6. **Independent confirmation after selection:** once a mechanism is fixed, validate on untouched levels/parents under the appropriate matched-work/soundness contract.

These are evidence-strength dimensions, not prerequisites for a cheap observer. The stronger the architectural claim, the more of the chain it should satisfy.

## First-loss evidence roles

Use the earliest point where **all known-live representative basins** have disappeared, while preserving `unknown` whenever the evidence cannot support a sharper label.

| Evidence role | Meaning | Default workstream consequence |
|---|---|---|
| `correctness-unsound` | A hard rule/cache/state computation rejects or corrupts a demonstrated live continuation. | Correctness fix immediately; do not treat as optimization research. |
| `allocation-exposure` | A demonstrated useful action/config never receives meaningful comparable work. | WS1/WS2 allocation/routing; no architecture claim. |
| `joint-feasibility-unrepresented` | Obligations/resources remain individually feasible to existing checks but their combination is already impossible or critically constrained in a recurring expressible way. | WS2 generic reasoning; joint-obligation observer is the first active test. |
| `residual-interface-opportunity` | Path history has consumed/competed for region interfaces, corridors, axes, portal transitions, or other future opportunities that current reasoning does not summarize adequately. | WS2 mechanism diagnosis; may later reopen WS4/WS5 depending on where loss occurs. |
| `state-equivalence-loss` | Distinct future-relevant states are merged/deduplicated/treated as equivalent by a search abstraction. | Reopen WS4 only with recurring evidence beyond already-closed salvage forms; WS5/reference evidence adjudicates liveness. |
| `rank-retention-loss` | Viable material survives legality/reasoning but is repeatedly score/beam/retention culled. | WS4 search retention/scoring; do not relabel as missing reasoning. |
| `repair-reachability` | The load-bearing viable change requires revising an interior/early commitment that current repair operators cannot reconstruct. | WS6, with operator reachability as the premise rather than another score/seed retune. |
| `unknown` | Evidence is incomplete, multiple mechanisms remain plausible, or stored basins are insufficient to support a claim. | Gather the smallest discriminating evidence; do not force routing. |

A level may carry more than one evidence role if distinct known-live basins die for different reasons. Aggregate results must report denominators and `unknown`; do not manufacture a single dominant label per level when the evidence is mixed.

### WS7 — architectural speed/execution substrate

No research-priority change. Optimize a future-feasibility mechanism only after it earns algorithmic value and its runtime cost is measured. Do not build expensive topology infrastructure speculatively.

### WS0 / WS3 / WS8

## Structural-response program: two legitimate exits

The structural technique-response ladder should no longer assume its only successful endpoint is routing:

- **Technique-relative exit:** a temporally stable, difficulty-controlled distinction explains why A responds better than B; continue through family/profile/trace evidence toward WS1 policy or technique-specific mechanism work.
- **Shared-capability exit:** multiple operationally distinct actions struggle at the same controlled structural/residual-state boundary and first-loss evidence identifies a recurring missing future-feasibility abstraction; route to WS2/specialist capability work.

A structural feature that merely predicts “hard” belongs to neither exit until mechanism evidence arrives.

## Reasoning-capability regression assets

Do not generate a new capability corpus now. When a shared mechanism earns implementation, preserve the smallest independent counterexamples/family transforms that establish its semantic boundary as regression/soundness assets. Examples may include one-vs-two usable interfaces, one unit of coupled intersection slack, or an isomorphic orientation exposing unintended dependence. New generated families require the ordinary variant-research value-of-information gate.

## Stop rules

- Do not call a stored path/basin “the only live future”; it proves liveness, not exclusivity.
- Do not reopen closed WS4/WS6/WS0 forms without a materially new mechanism premise.
- Do not respond to residual-topology evidence with another generic static topology bundle; the current question is dynamic future relevance.
- Do not build complement/Hamiltonian, separator, blackboard, ML scheduler or other framework code before a bounded recurring mechanism earns it.
- If the same alleged phenotype disappears under class-4/composition controls or varies idiosyncratically by action, record that negative and keep technique-level research primary.
