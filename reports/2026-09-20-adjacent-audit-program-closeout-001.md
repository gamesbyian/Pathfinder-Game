## What this program actually changed

The audit queue was not merely documentary. It produced or strengthened machine owners and live boundaries including:

- semantic/population identity ownership;
- exact repository-ref syntax;
- unit-topology vocabulary;
- explicit workstream/question lifecycle coherence;
- frozen analysis contracts and design authority;
- observer parity/reactivity interpretation;
- question-dossier applicability boundaries;
- family sibling-dependence semantics;
- context-budget authority hygiene;
- test assertions that target invariants rather than incidental prose.

## The second-pass correction

The first unit-topology audit claimed a broad category but concretely inspected mostly WS2/Class-3 material. The closure pass noticed that mismatch and reopened the audit against family/variant, hint/provenance, exact/reference and repeated-technique research.

That second pass found a real live seam in family-boundary output, which now declares parent-family dependence/generalization alongside row-weighted mutation summaries.

This is an important meta-result: **an audit report's scope claim is itself testable against what it actually inspected.** A clean first report does not close the category when its concrete evidence is narrower than its stated selection.

## What remains

The remaining gates in the individual reports are mostly one of three forms:

1. **prospective discipline** — apply the rule when a new study/field/observer appears;
2. **data-gated** — e.g. latent failure-response joins await an honest compatible population;
3. **promotion threshold** — extract a new shared primitive only after a second live consumer independently needs the same semantics.

Those are not reasons to keep this audit program open.

## Stop rule

Do not rerun these 15 audits as ceremony.

Reopen an area only when:

- a new concrete failure lands in the same class;
- a live consumer crosses one of the documented promotion thresholds;
- or a later closure pass shows that an audit's claimed scope exceeded what it actually examined.

Otherwise return attention to solver questions and new solves at acceptable budgets.
