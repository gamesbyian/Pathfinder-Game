## Frozen source and method

Machine snapshot:

- `reports/2026-09-19-research-portfolio-retrospective-data-001.json`

Rebuilder:

- `npm run research:portfolio-retrospective -- --start=2026-09-12 --end=2026-09-19`

The retrospective uses the **question relation** as its unit. A question enters the frozen window when its current structured relation retains at least one dated `answeredBy` or `constrainedBy` report reference inside the window.

This is intentionally not a commit count, agent-session count, report count, or researcher-hour estimate. Dated retained evidence is a bounded attention proxy only.

The answerability decomposition is a transparent screening heuristic over the owning question's state/result/reopen text. The owning relation remains authoritative.

## Is research attention measurably instrument-shaped?

Not in the strong sense hypothesized by the plan.

Only **5 of 28** recent questions carry an explicit Measurement Opportunity relation, using just three MOs:

- `MO-004` budget response / solve elasticity: 3 questions;
- `MO-002` rejection false-negative opportunity cost: 1 question;
- `MO-005` residual distance/progress representation: 1 question.

The other **23 of 28** recent questions are not formally attached to an MO.

That does **not** prove research is instrument-independent. Existing scripts, vocabularies, corpora and authority routing can shape attention without an MO tag. But the current data rejects the simplest version of the pathology: the formal MO system is not visibly acting as a narrow funnel through which most recent questions must pass.

The stronger interpretation is almost the reverse: formal MO coverage remains sparse relative to the actual question population. That makes MO association a poor proxy for total agenda formation today.

No persistent attention dashboard or automatic research scheduler is earned by this result.

## Answerability and missing research capabilities

The recent open/deferred portfolio does not show a broad shortage of research primitives.

The dominant blocker is **acquisition**:

- eligible compatible populations;
- prospective protocol-compatible telemetry;
- frozen independent transfer samples;
- dispatched probes.

This matters because it changes the intervention. Building another representation or analysis framework would not unblock most current questions.

The single clear measurement/construction gap is G1 complete-path LNS. Even there, the missing object is specific: construct a fair complete relaxed candidate from real production-search partials and measure local repairability. That is far narrower than a new generic LNS research framework.

The failure-response reconnaissance carries semantic/context concerns in its text, but its primary gate is still concrete data availability. The instrument and analysis contract already exist.

## Candidate/proposal provenance

Of the 28 recent questions:

- **14** retain an explicit `triggeredBy` relation;
- **17** visibly preserve a candidate/alternative/discriminator set in the question or current result text;
- **5** expose neither.

Those five are:

- `WS2-CLASS123-CHEAP-HARVEST`
- `WS2-KNOWN-CAPABILITY-COMPLEMENTARITY`
- `WS2-GENERIC-STRUCTURAL-DIVERSITY`
- `WS2-H2-STRUCTURAL-STASIS`
- `WS2-MECHANIC-COMPOSITION-CAUSALITY`

This is the one retrospective trigger that fires.

It is **not yet evidence that these questions came from an impoverished candidate grammar**. The current relation format may simply have failed to preserve how they were nominated. Treat the distinction as the point of the follow-up:

> Was the candidate set absent at decision time, or is its provenance merely absent from the current structured memory?

The cheapest next step is bounded archaeology on these five questions, not independent moonshot generation across the whole research program.

If the historical reports clearly preserve their nomination/candidate context, backfill or derive the provenance through existing relations/views where justified.

If they do not, then candidate-set absence becomes a real Bundle-G trigger and a small independent/non-exposed reconstruction is earned.

## Negative-result intersections

The machine retrospective found **no repeated structured intersection** among recent negative questions on the currently represented dimensions:

- shared MO;
- shared constraint ref;
- shared trigger ref.

That means Phase 23 does not currently nominate a clean shared-hidden-assumption cluster from structured question metadata alone.

This is a null about the represented relation grammar, not proof that negative results share no hidden assumption. Do not reopen closed forms to search for one.

## Independence and corroboration

This retrospective deliberately does not copy the architecture dependency graph into another dataset.

When a recent conclusion appears corroborated across multiple tools or producers, interpret it against:

`npm run research:system-inventory -- --view=architecture`

That view now exposes shared implementation dependencies and contract ownership. A later synthesis can therefore distinguish multiple artifacts from genuinely independent evidence without making E maintain a second dependency graph.

## What E earns, and what it does not

This first pass earns:

1. keeping the retrospective derivation as a cheap, reproducible diagnostic while it has an active consumer;
2. one bounded candidate-provenance archaeology pass over five named questions;
3. preserving decomposed answerability rather than treating all deferred questions as equally “hard”;
4. retaining MO-007 as research-process metrology without promoting it into a productivity score.

It does **not** earn:

- a permanent research-attention registry;
- an automatic priority score;
- a question scheduler;
- a new capability database;
- broad blind exploration;
- a standing independent-agent quota;
- automatic reopening of negative questions;
- a claim that formal instruments caused recent research attention.

## Bundle-E disposition

The main hypothesized pathology is not demonstrated strongly enough to justify new reflexivity machinery.

The more concrete finding is a **memory/provenance ambiguity** at question nomination: five recent questions do not currently let a machine distinguish “no competing candidates were considered” from “the structured relation simply forgot how this question arose.”

Resolve that bounded ambiguity first.

If it resolves as missing metadata, Bundle E can close with no Bundle-G exploration.

If it resolves as genuine candidate-set absence, use the cheapest triggered exploration method from Part VII, likely a small counterfactual-archaeology or controlled non-exposure reconstruction focused on those question families rather than the entire solver program.
