## Question and independent oracle controls

PG-N 001 had seven endpoint/depth mixed LIVE/DEAD groups; full visited support separated every unique path, rendering the proposed compression vacuous. The new cheap interface candidates preserve a minimal *local* path-history resource, rather than a full path-set identity. For each raw coordinate prefix they use:

- Endpoint, raw transition count and last transition axis (`H`, `V`, or `portal` if not adjacent).
- A fixed N/E/S/W four-neighbor **ever-visited mask**, or four two-bit **H/V consumed-axis** values. Each ordinary adjacent segment contributes its H/V bit to both endpoint cells. Long portal jumps contribute no artificial H/V consumption.

All signatures include the explicit corpus and parent puzzle ID. They are computed **before reading the exact fate**, using only prefix coordinates, and do not require hint data or solver control inputs. The source rows have declared 0 input/correctness alarms; every LIVE row has a referee-accepted solution and every DEAD row has CP-SAT `INFEASIBLE`. The new observer also enforces the source-file SHA-256s from PG-N 001, rejects contradictory exact labels, and checks the fixed 28/25/7 population shape.

This still cannot be the game's full mechanic state representation. In particular, proximity summaries omit remote consumed axes, visit multiplicity, exact crossing statuses, portal-use and global flipper state, future-edge coupling, and all other obligations. Even if many paths shared the key and had the same fate on known examples, that would not be enough for a sound exact cache or a prune.

The two surviving incoming-axis mixed groups occur at P00009 depth 6 and P00137 depth 10. The minimal four-neighbor footprint distinguishes both. But in each neighborhood signature, the **sole** repeated key is the same two P00148 exact-DEAD alternatives (`P00148-w0-A6-B10-alternate` and `P00148-w0-A5-B10-alternate`), whose paths differ in a short earlier detour. Both have raw depth 10, endpoint (2,4), identical incoming axis and locally equal adjacent-cell resources. No repeated LIVE/LIVE signature remains.

Thus the literal observed collision-free key compresses 25 sampled states to 24 keys, merging just two DEAD states. This is an **upper bound on the demonstrated merging opportunity in this selected cohort**, not an upper bound on another population's opportunities. It is also not exact future-equivalence: two separately DEAD prefixes need not share future continuations or a reusable proof.

Across the wider exploratory feature family, coarse adjacent occupancy counts retained mixed fates, while finer local masks largely drove signatures toward singletons. The reported forms are descriptive simplifications after that exploratory search. No held-out inference should be attached to their 0 mixed groups.

## Decision and next gate

**Close this compact-neighborhood equivalence attempt**, not the broader PG-N interface question. An optimizer needs a *repeated* typed fact that is safe, cheaper than recomputation and appears early enough to spare substantial **canonical** work. On 25 highly selected prefixes, the near-singleton keys supply none of that economic evidence.

For the next cheapest falsifier, freeze a modest independent-parent sample of **actual production-native retained prefixes** at strict typical work, without consulting oracle outcomes or historical winners. Count candidate-key repeated prefixes per independent parent, not every correlated frontier row as independent. If repeated support is absent, stop immediately. If present, use outcome-blind selection within those groups to request exact LIVE/DEAD labels, reject a candidate on any mixed exact group, require non-trivial independent repeated support and profile actual descendant canonical work before proposing a consumer. No broad state engine or large new CP-SAT campaign is warranted from this evidence.
