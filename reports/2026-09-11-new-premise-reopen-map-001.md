## Premise card A — controlled mechanic-composition transfer

### Evidence that changes the old premise

The old generator-expansion question was gated on a concrete within-corpus intervention surviving and materially needing cross-generator transfer. That condition is now met narrowly:

- must-cross neighbour-budget propagation previously promoted on portal+must-cross levels;
- the must-cross-forced-neighbour x visited-portal-terminal joint-obligation hard prune subsequently promoted at +21/-0 on its frozen 219-level opportunity population;
- the mechanism is not merely correlated with portal-heavy levels: it is a coupled obligation whose semantic boundary depends on the interaction of must-cross demand with a portal terminal that cannot be entered again.

### New premise

**Controlled families that vary portal/must-cross coupled-obligation geometry can test whether the promoted reasoning transfers across nearby semantic boundaries and can expose adjacent missing joint-feasibility rules.**

This is materially different from generating more hard portal levels. The independent variable is the coupled-obligation structure.

### Cheapest falsifying pilot

Use the smallest existing generator/family machinery capable of producing paired or grouped levels that preserve most geometry while changing one of:

- whether the forced must-cross neighbour is a previously consumed portal terminal;
- whether an alternative usable neighbour/interface remains;
- whether one unit of relevant obligation slack is added or removed;
- whether the portal terminal is revisitable under the mechanic state.

Prefer existing variant/family transforms if they can express the contrast. Add portal-aware generation only for contrasts that cannot be represented otherwise.

### Comparator / independence contract

- parent/family is the independent unit;
- preserve whole parents across discovery/confirmation splits;
- compare treatment-sensitive siblings, not an aggregate generated-vs-corpus solve rate;
- validate generated levels and any claimed solve with the ordinary referee/oracle path;
- do not use generated identity or family membership as a production routing input.

### Success gate

Escalate only if controlled structural flips reproducibly change observer/prune applicability or first-loss phenotype in the predicted direction and the mechanism remains level-blind and semantic, not family-ID dependent.

### Stop gate

Stop if controlled siblings do not track the proposed coupled-obligation distinction, if effects reduce to generic difficulty, or if the generator cannot isolate the semantic contrast without changing many confounders at once.

### Legal production path

A successful family program may justify one generic state-derived propagation/check. Generated families remain validation/research assets only.

## Premise card B — dynamic residual-interface / state-equivalence research (WS4)

### Current evidence

R01273 establishes that a coarse beam state can merge futures that diverge because of consumed path history. Short predecessor identity and bounded extra-survivor salvage delayed the first loss but did not converge, so those forms are correctly closed.

### New premise trigger

Reopen WS4 only if independent first-loss cases show that live material repeatedly disappears because current merge/retention logic fails to represent a **dynamic future-relevance property**, such as remaining corridor/interface capacity or opportunity already consumed by the path.

The new premise is not "remember more history." It is **summarize the future consequence of history**.

### Cheapest falsifying pilot after trigger

For a bounded recurrence set, compute one candidate residual descriptor offline/observer-only and ask whether it separates the known-live survivor from the state that currently displaces/merges it. Examples must be mechanism-earned, not chosen from a generic feature sweep.

### Success gate

Require recurrence on independent levels and materially different actions/configurations, then an untouched confirmation set. Only then test bounded retention or equivalence logic.

### Stop gate

If recurrence is confined to one level, one nearby beam configuration, or descriptors only postpone extinction without converging toward retained liveness, keep WS4 closed.

## Premise card C — repair operator reachability (WS6)

### New premise trigger

Reopen WS6 when first-loss/family evidence demonstrates that a valid continuation requires revising an early or interior commitment and current repair operators cannot construct that edit from their reachable mutation topology.

### Cheapest falsifying pilot after trigger

Take a small set of referee-valid source-controlled rescues and ask whether the required edit is reachable under the existing repair operators independent of scoring and seed order. Instrument/operator-enumerate reachability before adding any operator.

### Success gate

A recurring unreachable edit class must explain real failed repair opportunities across independent levels/families. A new operator must then show matched-work rescue benefit without correctness loss.

### Stop gate

If the edit is already operator-reachable and merely poorly ranked, budgeted, or sampled, route to search policy/allocation instead. Do not reopen WS6 for another score/seed/dose retune.

## Premise card D — commitment-boundary restart diversity (WS0)

### New premise trigger

Reopen WS0 only when first-loss evidence says viable alternatives exist but materially different runs/actions repeatedly collapse onto the same bad commitment before those alternatives are explored. Missing joint-feasibility reasoning, state-equivalence loss, and repair unreachability must remain separate diagnoses.

### Cheapest falsifying pilot after trigger

At the diagnosed commitment boundary, compare one bounded diversity intervention against the normal restart policy under fixed work. Candidate interventions must alter the commitment distribution, not merely add more seeds or work.

### Success gate

Show increased independent-basin coverage and treatment-exclusive solves at matched work on a frozen opportunity population, followed by untouched confirmation.

### Stop gate

No funding for global seed fan-out, whole-ladder retries, or nearby-threshold widening. If diversity changes but solve/work does not, close the tested intervention.

## Premise card E — mechanism-aware continuation / handoff

### New premise trigger

The null same-policy resumable-tranche result closed "continue the same thing longer." Reopen continuation/handoff only when there is either:

1. a genuinely distinct future policy with demonstrated complementarity; or
2. an earned state-selection mechanism that identifies partial states retaining known-live futures.

The second route is newly plausible because the first-loss program explicitly seeks residual-state future-feasibility phenotypes.

### Cheapest falsifying pilot after trigger

Offline, test whether the nominated descriptor ranks/filters known-live retained states above the states selected by the current continuation boundary. Only if that works should a matched-work continuation A/B be funded.

### Success gate

Require real treatment participation, fixed total work, preserved continuation ownership/state-detachment invariants, treatment-exclusive solves, and no losses before promotion.

### Stop gate

Do not retry tranche size, beam-policy menu growth, staged switching, or simple beam->DFS handoff without the new selection mechanism.

## Premise card F — one mechanism-specific selector

### New premise trigger

The failed 18-feature static bundle rules out generic feature accretion, not every future descriptor. A selector becomes eligible only when first-loss/family/trace evidence nominates one causal mechanism.

### Cheapest falsifying pilot after trigger

Implement the descriptor as offline analysis or observer-only instrumentation first. Test whether it separates the predicted first-loss/control cases after temporal and generic-difficulty controls.

### Success gate

Only a compact held-out signal tied to the nominated mechanism can graduate into WS1 routing or a generic capability check.

### Stop gate

No feature expansion after a null result. Do not reinterpret generic hardness prediction as action-selection value.
