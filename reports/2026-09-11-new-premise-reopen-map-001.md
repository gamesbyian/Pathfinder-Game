## Card A: controlled mechanic-composition transfer

Its +21/-0 frozen A/B result establishes real generic rescue capability, so adjacent semantic boundaries may now be tested causally.

**Premise:** controlled siblings that alter only the portal/must-cross coupled-obligation geometry can test whether the promoted reasoning transfers across nearby semantic boundaries and can expose adjacent missing joint-feasibility rules.

This line is no longer future work. The design authority is [`2026-09-11-mechanic-composition-transfer-pilot-design-001.md`](2026-09-11-mechanic-composition-transfer-pilot-design-001.md), and the live execution route is `docs/solver-optimization-workstreams.md`.

### Success / stop boundary

Advance only if multiple independent sibling pairs reproduce the predicted causal chain from structural decoupling through observer participation to changed prune rescue/cost. Stop if the edit cannot be cleanly reused, observer behaviour does not follow the manipulation, or connectivity/general-difficulty confounds dominate.

A positive pilot may earn one adjacent, separately prespecified contrast or a larger untouched confirmation cohort. It does not authorize broad generator expansion or generated-identity routing.

## Card B: dynamic residual-interface / state-equivalence research (WS4)

`R01273` establishes that a coarse beam state can merge futures that differ because of consumed history. Bounded predecessor/survivor variants delayed failure but did not converge.

Reopen WS4 only if independent first-loss cases show live material repeatedly disappearing because current merge/retention logic omits a dynamic **future consequence of history**, such as remaining interface capacity or already-consumed opportunity.

The cheapest pilot after such recurrence is observer/offline measurement of one mechanism-earned residual descriptor on a bounded set. Require recurrence across independent levels and materially different actions/configurations, then untouched confirmation before changing retention logic.

Keep WS4 closed if the effect remains one-level/one-beam-config specific or candidate descriptors merely postpone extinction.

## Card C: repair operator reachability (WS6)

Reopen WS6 only when a recurring referee-valid rescue requires an early/interior edit that current repair operators cannot construct and that operator incapability is the operative obstacle rather than exposure, ranking or budget.

The current first-loss frontier does **not** earn this reopening. Repair naturally fails to reach the relevant known-live states; when seeded exactly there, 4/28 are reconstructable and 24/28 hit the operator ceiling, but that reconstructability split is not materially different from the older general-population characterization. The novel evidence belongs to exposure/state selection, not generic operator-topology deficiency.

After a genuine trigger, enumerate/operator-test reachability before adding any operator. If the edit is already reachable but poorly sampled or ranked, route the problem to selection/allocation instead.

## Card D: commitment-boundary restart diversity (WS0)

Reopen WS0 only when viable alternatives demonstrably remain alive while materially different runs/actions repeatedly collapse onto the same bad commitment before exploring them.

Any pilot must alter the commitment distribution at the diagnosed boundary under fixed work. More seeds, more runtime, whole-ladder retries and nearby-threshold widening do not satisfy this premise.

Advance only on increased independent-basin coverage plus treatment-exclusive solves at matched work, followed by untouched confirmation.

## Card E: mechanism-aware continuation / handoff

The old same-policy resumable-tranche result closed “continue the same thing longer.” Reopen only for either:

1. a genuinely distinct future policy with demonstrated complementarity; or
2. an earned state-selection mechanism identifying partial states with retained live futures.

The first-loss/repair exposure study supplies the first nomination for route 2: at the exact state where beam loses known-live support, repair can complete 4/28 cases even though its natural search essentially never reaches those states. All four completions are referee/replay verified.

That is still **nomination only**. Before designing a handoff, size the phenomenon on a materially larger independent sample using the same natural-exposure plus operator-reachability-at-cull-depth measurement. Only then test offline whether the nominated descriptor ranks retained-live states above the current continuation boundary.

A later matched-work handoff must preserve continuation ownership/state-detachment invariants, show real treatment participation and treatment-exclusive solves, and incur no correctness loss. Do not retry tranche size, staged switching or simple beam->DFS handoff without the new selection mechanism.

## Card F: one mechanism-specific selector

The failed generic 18-feature static bundle rules out indiscriminate feature accretion, not every future descriptor. A selector becomes eligible only when first-loss/family/trace evidence nominates one causal mechanism.

Test the descriptor offline/observer-only first, after temporal and generic-difficulty controls. Only a compact held-out signal tied to the nominated mechanism can graduate into WS1 routing or a generic capability check. A null result does not justify expanding the feature bundle.
