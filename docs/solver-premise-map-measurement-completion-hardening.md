# Solver premise-map measurement-completion hardening

> **Status:** concluded-positive
> **Last evidence:** 2026-09-17 — bounded audit reconciled candidate missing observables against canonical v2, independent peer-map construction, shadow mining, and cross-reconciliation.
> **Decision:** add a non-premise measurement-opportunity representation; do not create new premise IDs or reopen broad mining.
> **Remaining gate:** integrate this overlay into the later representation-hardening / successor-map landing pass and preserve its distinction from canonical premise admission.

## Question

Does the premise-map program materially under-represent a class of research object of the form:

> This currently unmeasured observable could distinguish live rival explanations or expose a hidden structure that the existing premises leave ambiguous.

The answer is **partly**.

The current map is already strong on:

- measurement validity;
- participation and attribution;
- causal discriminability;
- evidence authority and maturity;
- instrumentation missingness;
- opportunity populations;
- residual formation;
- first-loss causality;
- composition and synergy;
- budget / scheduler dependence;
- experimental primitives.

The gap is narrower: the map does not consistently preserve the **specific discriminating observable itself** as a first-class, non-premise research object linked to the premises it could distinguish.

That is a representation gap, not evidence of a missing conceptual continent.

## Why this is not a new premise family

Canonical v2 already contains P204:

> An observation supports a causal research conclusion only to the extent that the observation could discriminate that conclusion from materially different rival explanations.

P204 supplies the governing epistemic rule.

Phase-2 mining already records "smallest discriminator / primitive" for many synthesis candidates.

The independent peer map includes "Measurement must match the causal proposition."

Shadow mining independently emphasizes that evidence is endogenously selected by the process that produces it.

Therefore the new representational object should be:

`measurement opportunity -> live rival explanations / mapped premises -> discriminating observable -> evidence role`

rather than:

`measurement opportunity -> new canonical premise ID`

## Representation rule

A measurement opportunity deserves first-class recording only when all of the following hold:

1. at least two live mapped explanations, scopes, or mechanism classes remain materially confounded;
2. the proposed observable has a credible path to changing belief between them;
3. the observable is not already decision-equivalent to retained telemetry;
4. collecting it would not silently assume the intervention it is intended to evaluate;
5. its population, censoring, observer effect, work cost, and information authority can be stated;
6. the result would change a research decision, reopen condition, or experimental design.

Interesting telemetry that does not meet these conditions remains an idea, not map hardening.

## Candidate dispositions

### Search-state / trajectory overlap between solver regimes

**Disposition:** already represented semantically; retain as a measurement specialization, not a new premise.

Relevant canonical territory:

- P167: operational-similarity measurements are policy-conditioned and trace-censored;
- P199: the correct generalization unit may be a trajectory rather than a static level feature;
- P154: execution/predecessor context can change action value.

The potentially useful measurement is overlap/divergence in reached states or trajectories under matched context and work.

Its value is diagnostic: distinguish genuinely different capability from differently named policies that traverse effectively the same search region, or distinguish late ranking differences from early basin divergence.

This is a good example of a measurement opportunity that does not need a premise ID.

### False-negative opportunity cost of heuristic pruning or rejection

**Disposition:** conceptually covered; retain as a counterfactual measurement specialization.

Relevant canonical territory:

- P030: current gating/pruning may suppress unique valid solves;
- P031: production prunes must remain sound;
- P183: first loss should be tied to a minimal counterfactual decision change;
- P204: causal conclusions require rival-discriminating observations.

The discriminating observable is not merely "prune fired." It is whether rejected alternatives contained unique or materially better continuation opportunity under an independently justified reference/witness/counterfactual.

This should not be generalized into an assumption that every heuristic rejection needs exact counterfactual proof.

### Non-additive interaction / synergy between solver mechanisms

**Disposition:** already explicit at premise level; measurement opportunity clarifies operational test.

Relevant canonical territory:

- P133: interactions among individually weak mechanisms can create capability unavailable to either alone;
- P192: new solves may arise from compositions/sequences whose credit cannot be assigned faithfully to one named technique.

A useful observable is the interaction residual under matched work:

`effect(A+B) - expected effect under an explicitly stated additive/null composition model`

with solve-set churn and independent opportunity population retained.

No new semantic premise is required.

### Full budget-response / solve-elasticity curves

**Disposition:** budget dependence is strongly represented; the curve itself is a useful measurement primitive.

Relevant canonical territory:

- P123: work/budget comparability is nontrivial;
- P154: action value is execution-context conditioned;
- P187: capability is non-monotone under a fixed envelope.

Single-budget A/B evidence can leave distinct explanations confounded:

- capability absent;
- capability present but underdosed;
- capability positive only after a threshold;
- capability displaced at larger budgets;
- portfolio order changing the marginal curve.

A bounded multi-budget response curve can discriminate these cases.

Do not elevate "always measure full curves" into a governing requirement. Curves are warranted where budget-conditioned rival explanations remain live.

### Quantitative distance-to-solution representations for residuals

**Disposition:** genuine under-explicit measurement opportunity; no premise ID yet.

The map contains related ingredients:

- first-loss causality;
- witness-relative lineage;
- information value versus immediate progress;
- near-miss candidate-source census;
- residual formation;
- topology and obligation diagnostics.

But there is no equally explicit representation of a **distance/progress observable** whose purpose is to stratify residual states by how close they are to a valid completion under a stated semantic/reference model.

This could distinguish:

- structurally near-complete but misranked states;
- states with many superficial progress signals but no viable completion basin;
- generation failures from late search-allocation failures;
- mechanisms that improve latent progress without yet changing terminal solve count.

"Distance to solution" is not one obvious scalar. Candidate forms may include exact/reference-model distance, witness edit/decision distance, obligation deficit, remaining causal commitments, or probabilistic completion headroom.

The hardening action is to preserve this as a measurement opportunity, not to canonize one metric prematurely.

### Covariance/clustering of gain-loss patterns across experiments

**Disposition:** parent ideas already exist; measurement formulation is under-explicit.

Relevant canonical territory:

- P003: zero unique-solve delta does not imply capability uselessness;
- P133/P192: interaction/compositional capability;
- P199: the correct generalization unit may be a latent/dynamic class;
- P201: apparent support must respect clustered/independent units.

A cross-experiment gain/loss covariance structure could reveal latent capability classes not named by current mechanic or level-feature taxonomies.

However, clustering is especially vulnerable to:

- shared population ancestry;
- portfolio-conditioned residuals;
- correlated experiments;
- arbitrary distance metrics;
- post-hoc class naming.

Therefore this is a measurement opportunity only when causal ancestry and opportunity populations are preserved.

No new latent-class premise is earned merely by the existence of clustering machinery.

### Research-process yield / premise fertility

**Disposition:** genuine metrology-layer candidate, but belongs to research-process measurement rather than solver premise space unless it becomes decision-bearing.

The current program measures evidence quality and research validity extensively, but only weakly represents the productivity of the research process itself.

Potential observables include:

- experiments required per independently supported premise refinement;
- information gain / entropy reduction across live rival explanations;
- fraction of experiments that materially change a research decision;
- development work per new independently confirmed capability;
- development work per retained new solve after confirmation;
- premise reopen/closure churn;
- number of downstream tests generated by one premise versus number that collapse as duplicates.

These metrics can easily become Goodhart targets and should not be turned into one research score.

The only legitimate use is diagnostic: identify research areas that consume substantial effort while repeatedly failing to reduce uncertainty or create independent capability evidence.

This remains a measurement opportunity, not a canonical solver premise.

## Saturation result

Five of the seven candidate families substantially reduce to existing premises plus a more explicit observable:

- trajectory overlap;
- pruning/rejection false-negative opportunity;
- interaction/synergy;
- budget response;
- gain/loss covariance.

Two remain notably under-explicit as measurements:

- residual distance/progress representation;
- research-process yield / premise fertility.

Neither currently earns a canonical premise ID.

This is therefore a **partial saturation result**:

> The conceptual parents are mostly already present. What was missing was a durable representational place for discriminating observables that sit between a premise and an experiment.

## Relationship to existing experimental primitives

Measurement opportunities are adjacent to, but not identical with, experimental primitives.

An experimental primitive is the smallest tooling/telemetry/intervention needed to run a discriminator.

A measurement opportunity is the epistemic object that states:

- which ambiguity exists;
- which observable could reduce it;
- why current evidence cannot;
- what result classes would change interpretation.

One measurement opportunity may require several possible primitives. One primitive may serve several opportunities.

## Relationship to P204

P204 remains the governing premise.

The measurement-opportunity layer operationalizes P204 without duplicating it.

P204 says evidence should discriminate rivals.

The overlay says **what observable is currently missing for a specific ambiguity**.

## Relationship to map-v3 preparation

This pass does not independently earn a v3 snapshot.

It should be consumed by the broader representation-hardening / v3-preparation work as one possible overlay dimension.

If a successor map is created, measurement opportunities should remain separately typed from canonical premises so that:

- telemetry ideas do not inflate premise counts;
- unimplemented observables do not masquerade as beliefs;
- saturation can be recorded explicitly;
- a future mining pass can ask which ambiguities lack discriminating observables without recursively treating every metric idea as a premise.

## Stop rule

Do not expand this register by brainstorming telemetry.

Add an entry only when a live mapped ambiguity exists and the candidate observable passes the six representation rules above.

A null audit that finds no useful observable for an unresolved region is itself informative and may indicate:

- the ambiguity is not empirically separable under current architecture;
- the map has split one premise too finely;
- an intervention rather than more observation is required;
- the question is not decision-bearing.
