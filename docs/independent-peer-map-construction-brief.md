# Independent peer-map construction brief

> **Mode:** blind reconstruction continuation.
> **Goal:** turn the existing independent premise-space investigation on this lineage into a sufficiently complete peer conceptual map for later comparison.
> **Critical rule:** do not compare against or inspect the recent canonical premise-map lineage until this investigation is formally closed.

## Allowed evidence

Work from the underlying Pathfinder repository and the evidence already used or naturally available to this independent lineage:

- solver source and historical code;
- git history that predates the recent canonical premise-map program;
- older research documentation and authorities;
- experiments and reports;
- scripts and research tooling;
- corpora and generator definitions;
- the existing independent investigation material already present on this branch.

Methodological hygiene such as provenance, uncertainty, alternative explanations, explicit source coverage, and separating discovery from later reconciliation is allowed.

## Quarantine

During construction, do not inspect other recent premise-map branches, PRs, inventories, ontologies, relation graphs, mining reports, reconciliations, or downstream conclusions.

Do not use another map's premise IDs, dimensions, relation vocabulary, or gap structure as a template.

Do not ask what the other map found and then search for analogues.

Do not reconcile while constructing.

## Deliverable

Produce a genuine peer conceptual object, not merely another prose notebook.

It should be complete enough to compare later on:

- conceptual coverage;
- premise/question boundaries;
- decomposition and aggregation choices;
- abstraction levels;
- relation/dependency structure;
- implicit defaults;
- negative conclusions and reopen conditions;
- evidence, scope, authority, and temporal treatment;
- unresolved regions and difficult-to-represent material.

The peer map may invent its own ontology, IDs, relation types, and storage format. Schema compatibility with another map is not a goal.

## Stop rule

Close the independent construction before looking at any comparison material.

At closeout record:

- exact branch head;
- evidence surfaces inspected;
- known blind spots;
- method changes;
- unresolved ambiguities;
- confirmation that no recent canonical premise-map or mining conclusions were inspected during this phase.

Do not mutate a canonical map, produce queue items, or begin map-vs-map reconciliation on this branch.
