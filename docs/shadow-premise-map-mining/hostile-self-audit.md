## 3. Frozen-input methodology contamination

Before the independent method was frozen, research-object inspection opened:

- `docs/solver-premise-map-hardening.md`;
- `docs/solver-premise-map-hardening-overlay.json`.

**Result:** contamination confirmed and preserved in `contamination-log.md`.

Severity: medium for the experimental claim “method invented with zero prior methodological exposure”; low-to-medium for the narrower claim “no prior mining conclusions or later lineage contaminated the substantive findings.”

## Remaining uncertainty

- Method independence is not pristine because of the frozen hardening-lens exposure.
- The map may omit reversal criteria or relations that exist in source documents; this investigation intentionally did not turn into a full source archaeology.
- The six-pass scheme is one plausible independent interrogation, not a proof that all higher-order structure in the map was exhausted.
- Some synthesis convergence may arise because several late propositions were themselves written as abstractions over earlier regions.
- Standard CSV field-count defects could affect any external tooling differently depending on its parser tolerance.

## Audit verdict

The branch constitutes a credible quarantined interrogation of the fixed conceptual object **with one disclosed methodological-exposure caveat**. It is suitable for later comparison provided that comparison treats the shadow result as independently executed but not as methodologically blind to every label embedded in the frozen hardening substrate.
